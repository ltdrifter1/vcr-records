Forsberg

Forsberg makes lo-fi house and UK garage built around dusty samples, wonky drums and rare grooves. Inspired by cozy underground clubs and music from all corners of the globe, he’s always digging for overlooked gems and unlikely sounds to flip into something new. Originally from Vancouver and now based in Cumberland, BC, he’s a lifelong record digger with a background in radio and a soft spot for music that feels a little left of centre. Built for intimate dancefloors, good friends and taking the long way home.
