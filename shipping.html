Orders

Shipping & returns

Clear terms before you check out. If something is unclear, write us.

Shipping

Orders ship from British Columbia, Canada. Most merch and vinyl leave within 2–4 business days. You will receive tracking when the parcel is scanned.

  • Domestic Canada: typically 3–7 business days after dispatch.
  • United States: typically 5–12 business days after dispatch.
  • International: usually 1–4 weeks, depending on customs.

Shipping is calculated at payment based on destination and weight. Digital purchases (when fulfilled via Bandcamp or Stripe) are delivered instantly — no shipping applies.

Packaging

Vinyl ships in protective mailers with corner protection. Apparel is folded and bagged. We pack light and carefully — the goal is the record arriving the way it left.

Returns

Unopened merch and sealed vinyl may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Return shipping is the buyer’s responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Opened vinyl and worn apparel cannot be returned except for manufacturing defects. Digital downloads are non-refundable once delivered.

To start a return, email charlie@vcrrecords.com with your order number and reason.

Damaged or lost

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the outer packaging and the item and contact us within 7 days. We will replace or refund at our discretion. Lost packages with active tracking are investigated with the carrier before a replacement is issued.

Authenticity

Everything sold in the Club Copy shop is produced or pressed for the label. Release numbers (CC00x) identify each title in the library.
