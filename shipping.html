Shipping & returns

Clear terms before you check out. If something is unclear, write us.

Shipping

Orders ship from British Columbia, Canada. Most merch and cassettes leave within 2–4 business days. You will receive tracking when the parcel is scanned.

Canada
$8 · Tracked · 3–7 business days after dispatch
United States
$14 · Tracked · 5–12 business days after dispatch
Digital
$0 · Delivered by email

Physical orders ship to Canada and the United States only. Choose a rate at Stripe Checkout. Cassettes ship in protective mailers. Apparel is folded and bagged.

Returns

Unopened merch and sealed cassettes may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Opened cassettes and worn apparel cannot be returned except for manufacturing defects. Digital downloads are non-refundable once delivered.

To start a return, email charlie@clubcopy.ca with your order number and reason.

Damaged or lost

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the outer packaging and the item and contact us within 7 days. We will replace or refund at our discretion. Lost packages with active tracking are investigated with the carrier before a replacement is issued.

Authenticity

Everything sold in the Club Copy shop is produced or pressed for the label. Each title in the library is an edition you can hear and keep.
