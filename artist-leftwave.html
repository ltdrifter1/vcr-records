Leftwave

Artist Electronic Atmosphere
Bandcamp Instagram

Leftwave arrives as a quieter current inside the VCR ecosystem — textures first, rhythm second, emotion always close to the surface.

Where pressure builds elsewhere on the label, Leftwave leans into space: soft distortion, submerged melodies, and electronic forms that feel cinematic without losing the underground edge. The project sits naturally beside VCR’s cassette culture — music that rewards listening in full, not fragments.
