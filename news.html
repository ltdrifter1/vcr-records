Stars for the Floor

The chart

Swipe · 1 / 12

  1. Ariesmar 21 – apr 19

    Do the task you keep moving to Monday. Block an hour, finish it, and leave the argument about how long it took.

  2. Taurusapr 20 – may 20

    Check the account before you buy anything for December. One useful purchase is enough. The rest can wait.

  3. Geminimay 21 – jun 20

    Return the call you have been avoiding, or say you will not. An open thread is what keeps you up.

  4. Cancerjun 21 – jul 22

    Make one plan that stays at home. A meal, a record, and a person you actually like. You do not have to go out to have a night.

  5. Leojul 23 – aug 22

    Ask for the thing by name: the job, the money, or the night. Then stop talking and let the other person answer.

  6. Virgoaug 23 – sep 22

    Close one loop: the form, the repair, or the pile by the door. Finished once beats a list of improvements.

  7. Librasep 23 – oct 22

    Send one invitation with a day and a time. If they cannot make it, pick another day. Do not keep three drafts.

  8. Scorpiooct 23 – nov 21

    Have the private conversation this week. One person, no audience, and no recap after.

  9. Sagittariusnov 22 – dec 21

    Book the ticket, the class, or the night away, and put the date where you will see it. Talking about it is not the same as going.

  10. Capricorndec 22 – jan 19

    Set the hour you are finished for the day, and leave the work there. Write the money coming in and the money going out before you look at next month.

  11. Aquariusjan 20 – feb 18

    Choose the night and tell one person. Anyone else can hear it from them. You do not have to wait for a vote.

  12. Piscesfeb 19 – mar 20

    Sleep at the hour you named, twice this week. Eat before you go out. Leaving when you said you would is the whole assignment.
