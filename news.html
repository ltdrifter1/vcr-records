Essay

  1. Surrender The Chemical Brothers released Surrender on 21 June 1999, on their own Freestyle Dust. A club duo who could not sing hired people who could, one track at a time. 23 Oct 2026
  2. Play Mute released Moby’s Play on 17 May 1999. It set Alan Lomax’s field singers inside electronic songs, then licensed every track. The singers were not the ones who got paid. 23 Oct 2026
  3. Get Ready! Get Ready for This began as a white-label instrumental in Antwerp in May 1991. The voices, the British edit, and the sports arenas came after. 23 Oct 2026
  4. Ray of Light Ray of Light, February 1998, is a William Orbit record. Madonna hired the Guerilla and Strange Cargo producer and refused to sand his machines down. 23 Oct 2026
  5. Homework Daft Punk’s debut left a Paris flat in January 1997. The 12-inch that mattered, Da Funk, had been out on Soma since May 1995. 23 Oct 2026
  6. The Fat of the Land XL released The Prodigy’s The Fat of the Land on 30 June 1997. It went to number one in Britain and America. Then Liam Howlett tried to blow the popularity up with one title. 23 Oct 2026
  7. Illmatic Nas’s debut, Columbia, 19 April 1994, was built by Premier, Large Professor, Pete Rock, Q-Tip and L.E.S. The shipments took years to catch the reputation. 23 Oct 2026
  8. Revibed Isn't Digging Revibed has sold digital rips of rare records since 2024 and called it caretaking. Terre Thaemlitz told them to stop. Resident Advisor reported the charge on 22 December 2025. 23 Oct 2026
  9. The Rooms Opened On 19 July 2021 England let the nightclubs open again. Fabric had about 900 people in. A vaccine pass was not required. A lateral-flow test got you a shorter queue. 23 Oct 2026
  10. They Settled It at Afterhours Madonna clapped back at Charli xcx’s dead-dancefloor line. Then they wrote the afters together. 17 Oct 2026
  11. No Undo On 7 October 2026 Jesper Kouthoofd told Highsnobiety he plans to stop making synthesizers, OP-1 included. DJ Mag wrote it up on 15 October. 16 Oct 2026

The gossip is the sound. The story is the room. 17 Oct 2026
  2. Eight Rooms, One Basement From 2008 to 2012 the Guvernment was eighth in the world. Toronto’s other night was a basement under the Silver Dollar, and a tent in a park. 17 Oct 2026

A band that began on home-made cassettes got a Sub Pop budget, a studio and, by Barlow's account, a drummer problem. What is an album worth to the person who made it? 10 Oct 2026
