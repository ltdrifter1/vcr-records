Athens After the Conservatory

Ioannis Rigas learned to wait for a note. Then he found rooms that only care if you can dance.

posted by cj archer · 04 aug 26

athenshousesigned 26

I put one on walking to the corner store and came home empty-handed.

Ioannis Rigas still plays like somebody who spent years in a room with a piano and a clock. National Conservatory of Chalandri, Athens. Classical first. Patience as a habit, not a brand.

What we signed is the 6am thing. The track that stays in your legs after the room has thinned and nobody left is performing for anybody. keep reading →
