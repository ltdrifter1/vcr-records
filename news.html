  1. 13SurrenderEssay · 23 Oct 2026The Chemical Brothers released Surrender on 21 June 1999, on their own Freestyle Dust. A club duo who could not sing hired people who could, one track at a time.
  2. 14PlayEssay · 23 Oct 2026Mute released Moby’s Play on 17 May 1999. It set Alan Lomax’s field singers inside electronic songs, then licensed every track. The singers were not the ones who got paid.
  3. 15Get Ready!Essay · 23 Oct 2026Get Ready for This began as a white-label instrumental in Antwerp in May 1991. The voices, the British edit, and the sports arenas came after.
  4. 16Ray of LightEssay · 23 Oct 2026Ray of Light, February 1998, is a William Orbit record. Madonna hired the Guerilla and Strange Cargo producer and refused to sand his machines down.
  5. 17HomeworkEssay · 23 Oct 2026Daft Punk’s debut left a Paris flat in January 1997. The 12-inch that mattered, Da Funk, had been out on Soma since May 1995.
  6. 18The Fat of the LandEssay · 23 Oct 2026XL released The Prodigy’s The Fat of the Land on 30 June 1997. It went to number one in Britain and America. Then Liam Howlett tried to blow the popularity up with one title.
  7. 19IllmaticEssay · 23 Oct 2026Nas’s debut, Columbia, 19 April 1994, was built by Premier, Large Professor, Pete Rock, Q-Tip and L.E.S. The shipments took years to catch the reputation.

Swipe · 1 / 12

  1. Ariesmar 21 – apr 19

    Wear the boots you’d climb a headland in, even to a basement. If the door looks twice, the fire saw you first.

  2. Taurusapr 20 – may 20

    Twelve dollars at the Saturday market, a ring with a crooked stone, and it’s yours. The one haggling on their phone doesn’t get that price, or that blessing.

  3. Geminimay 21 – jun 20

    Two crystals in one pocket. The darker one is for the message you keep drafting and not sending.

  4. Cancerjun 21 – jul 22

    Put the kettle on before you leave, with nettle or chamomile or whatever the garden gave you. The chipped mug is saved for whoever drifts home with you.

  5. Leojul 23 – aug 22

    Your name’s on a list you wrote in your own hand. Walk in like the list was a prophecy someone overheard.

  6. Virgoaug 23 – sep 22

    Let the crossed-out line on the set list stay. Do your face in the back seat by moonlight and trust the mirror less than the moon.

  7. Librasep 23 – oct 22

    Pass the aux on the Malahat. You argued for that song for six switchbacks, and the mountain didn’t care.

  8. Scorpiooct 23 – nov 21

    If they ask who played, describe the hands and the smoke. Keep the name folded in your pocket with the lighter and the sage.

  9. Sagittariusnov 22 – dec 21

    The last ferry is a rumour, but the rain is real. Walk the long way home while your hair still looks like you chose it.

  10. Capricorndec 22 – jan 19

    Press last night’s wristband into your journal like a flower. Your memory of it will be softer than the paper.

  11. Aquariusjan 20 – feb 18

    Three of them are still typing, and you’re already barefoot at the tideline. Send the pin, not another poll.

  12. Piscesfeb 19 – mar 20

    One drop of cedar oil behind the ear. The kitchen light doesn’t get a say in how you glow.
