Riscape, from Athens

Conservatory piano, then the rooms that empty at 7am.

posted by cj archer · 04 aug 26

Melody first, peak-time second. House from Athens — not the postcard.

Riscape is Ioannis Rigas, a producer and DJ from Athens. He started on classical piano at the National Conservatory of Chalandri, then moved into clubs and electronic production. The records keep that training in the room.

He began releasing in 2020, joined Mystery Freedom Records in 2021, and this year hit Beatport No. 1 with his remix of “Synth Magic.” Club Copy signed him because the tracks stay in the body after the night is over. keep reading →
