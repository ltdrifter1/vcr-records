# Club Copy — Redesign, October 2026

**Brief:** a full redesign. Reset the identity from what the best underground electronic labels do, keep the name, the mark, the CC numbering, the content, and every working feature (on-site streaming, cart and Stripe checkout, Record Club, Night Shift, mixtapes, the floor). A Node build script generates every page from the data files so the design applies everywhere at once.

**Reference family (chosen):** Hessle Audio, Timedance, Livity Sound, AD 93. Studied directly: Numbers (Glasgow), Timedance (`timedance.music`), AD 93 (`ad93.ltd`), Livity Sound and Timedance on Bandcamp, Boomkat as the archetypal UK catalogue interface. Hessle Audio's site refuses automated access, so it is covered from Bandcamp and from memory.

**What they share**

1. **One typeface.** A neutral grotesk, used at two or three sizes. No display face.
2. **The catalogue is the site.** Rows or a grid with the catalogue number as a first-class field. No hero banners, no sliders, no "featured" chrome.
3. **Mono metadata.** Catalogue number, format, year, date, in a monospace or small-caps line.
4. **A flat ground.** Pure black or pure white. No gradients, bezels, glass, grain, shadows or radii.
5. **Nothing moves.** Hover underlines at most.
6. **Shop is a word in the nav,** not a department.
7. **The footer carries the facts:** distribution, contact, newsletter, copyright span.

**What Club Copy has that they do not**

- A real on-site player with a persistent dock (Numbers, Timedance and AD 93 send you to Bandcamp).
- A monthly magazine with 128 pieces.
- A record club with tiers and a member card.
- Long DJ mixes with SoundCloud playback.
- A Pacific Northwest roster with photographs.

The redesign keeps all five and presents them with the references' discipline.

## 2. Design system

### Ground
Black throughout. `#0A0A0A` page, `#F0EFEA` ink, `#9A9A94` second ink, hairlines `rgba(255,255,255,.14)`. One accent, `#FF4A1C`, used only for state: on air, the active row, a focused control, a hover underline. Night Shift stories are typeset on the same black with a wider measure and larger body; the site is one surface.

### Type
- **Archivo** (variable, width axis 62–125). Expanded widths for display, normal for body, condensed for dense data. One family, three widths, no second face.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for catalogue data: numbers, formats, dates, prices.
- Scale: display `clamp(44px, 7vw, 112px)` at weight 600, width 112, tracking −0.03em; section `clamp(28px, 3.4vw, 48px)`; body 17/1.55; small 14; mono 12 uppercase tracking 0.08em.

### Layout
- Full-width rows separated by hairlines. 24 px gutters on phones, 40 px on desktop, no max-width on rows; text measures capped at 64 characters.
- Release row (Numbers): artist, title, mono line, actions on the left; cover on the right at 160–280 px.
- Catalogue (AD 93 + Timedance): a text list with cover thumbnails, filters by genre and artist, a sleeves view.
- No radii above 2 px. No shadows. No gradients. No backdrop filters.

### Motion
Hover underline, row hover lifts the cover 2 px, dock slides up. Everything else is instant. Reduced-motion respected.

### Components (new CSS, one file `css/cc.css`)
Nav, drawer, footer, row, catalogue row, filters, play key, buttons (two: solid and outline), forms, dock + Now Playing stage + bumper + toast + play gate (markup generated by `js/player.js`), mix card, floor frame, artist row, zine index, story body, volume page, archive table, shop grid, product page, cart, account, member card, 404.

## 4. Build

`node scripts/build-site.js` reads `data/*.json` and `content/news/*.html` and writes every page. No dependencies. The generated HTML is committed, so Vercel serves static files exactly as before. The three previous Night Shift builders and the SEO pass are retired; their outputs are produced by the one script.

One-time migrations (committed with their outputs): release notes and specs out of the 27 release pages into `catalog.json`; story bodies out of `news/*.html` into `content/news/`; product data out of `merch.html` and `merch/*.html` into `data/merch.json`; page-bound inline scripts into `js/`.
