03Melodic House · Athens2 releases

Riscape

Raised on classical piano before finding his way into clubs and electronic music, Athens-based producer Riscape blends melodic songwriting with peak-time energy. After studying piano at Chalandri's National Conservatory and later refining his production skills at SAE Athens and Grey Studios, he began releasing music in 2020.

Since then, Riscape has built a steady catalog of Melodic House, joining Dutch imprint Mystery Freedom Records in 2021 and releasing standout projects including the Unity and Two x S EPs. In 2026, he scored his first Beatport No. 1 on the Trance (Main Floor) Top 100 New Releases chart with his remix of "Synth Magic" by Alphed Le Cornett & JARR Head.

Away from the studio, Riscape curates Riscape Reality Radio, a mix series showcasing the records shaping his sound alongside his own productions. His music has earned support from respected playlists including Spinnin' Records, Sirup Music, and EDM Joy, while receiving airplay, blog coverage, and backing from DJs across the global electronic scene.

Balancing classical foundations with modern dancefloor instincts, Riscape continues to craft emotionally driven club music built for late nights, big systems, and lasting moments.

04Downtempo hip-hop

DW

DW is drawn to the softer side of hip-hop. Inspired by chill out rooms and downtempo beats. It's part jazz-room daydream, part basement beat tape, his sound brings together off-kilter samples, loose drums and the kind of melodies that stay with you long after the record stops. Unpolished by design, made for slow afternoons, late nights and getting lost in the music.
