Gorilla

Little Simz, a gorilla at the sampler, and the kind of edit you put on with the window cracked.

posted by cj archer · 08 sep 26

Same afternoon L.T. Drifta dropped fifteen instrumentals. Nobody asked them to match. They did anyway.

Molly Haze lives in Cumberland, which is the kind of island town you only reach if you meant to. Gorilla is her Little Simz edit — two minutes forty-four, the ape at the MPC like she’s been waiting for that bar all year. I played it in the kitchen with the rain doing that Pacific thing where it never quite commits to a storm.
