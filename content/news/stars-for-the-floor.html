I used to read the stars off the back page of a magazine someone left on the Swartz Bay ferry. The perfume sample was half peeled, and the typeface felt like a note from an aunt who knew things. Nobody on that boat believed in Mercury, but everybody turned to the page before they went out.

Each sign is one practice: a thing to do before the night starts, and the planet that wants it done. Do that thing. The planet is the reason.

If your sign feels wrong, read the next one and do that practice. The door is not checking birth charts. It only needs you to arrive with the rite already done.
