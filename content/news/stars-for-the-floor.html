I used to read the stars off the back page of a magazine someone left on the Swartz Bay ferry. The perfume sample was half peeled, and the typeface felt like a note from an aunt who knew things. Nobody on that boat believed in Mercury, but everybody turned to the page before they went out to hear someone.

There’s still sand in one boot by the door, and the lip balm on the windowsill never got its lid back. That’s the only calendar this paper trusts.
