01Lofi House · Vancouver, BC17 releases

Forsberg

Forsberg makes lo-fi house and UK garage built around dusty samples, wonky drums and rare grooves. Inspired by cozy underground clubs and music from all corners of the globe, he’s always digging for overlooked gems and unlikely sounds to flip into something new. Originally from Vancouver and now based in Cumberland, BC, he’s a lifelong record digger with a background in radio and a soft spot for music that feels a little left of centre. Built for intimate dancefloors, good friends and taking the long way home.

02Jungle / DNB · Cumberland, BC11 releases

LT Drifta

LT Drifta draws on the raw energy of 90s jungle, breakbeat hardcore and early rave, reworking the sounds of a bygone era into something fresh. Built around chopped breaks, soulful samples and hazy atmospheres, the project bridges the roots of jungle with a new generation of forward-thinking, liquid-leaning sounds.

With a love for exploring music’s past and finding new possibilities in forgotten sounds, LT brings an old-school sensibility to modern production. Nostalgic without being stuck in the past, it’s jungle music for modern living: paying respect to the foundations while pushing them somewhere new.

Artist page Latest: For My Sins

03Melodic House · Athens2 releases

Riscape

Raised on classical piano before finding his way into clubs and electronic music, Athens-based producer Riscape blends melodic songwriting with peak-time energy. After studying piano at Chalandri's National Conservatory and later refining his production skills at SAE Athens and Grey Studios, he began releasing music in 2020.

Since then, Riscape has built a steady catalog of Melodic House, joining Dutch imprint Mystery Freedom Records in 2021 and releasing standout projects including the Unity and Two x S EPs. In 2026, he scored his first Beatport No. 1 on the Trance (Main Floor) Top 100 New Releases chart with his remix of "Synth Magic" by Alphed Le Cornett & JARR Head.

Away from the studio, Riscape curates Riscape Reality Radio, a mix series showcasing the records shaping his sound alongside his own productions. His music has earned support from respected playlists including Spinnin' Records, Sirup Music, and EDM Joy, while receiving airplay, blog coverage, and backing from DJs across the global electronic scene.

Balancing classical foundations with modern dancefloor instincts, Riscape continues to craft emotionally driven club music built for late nights, big systems, and lasting moments.

Artist page Latest: Tell Me

04Downtempo hip-hop

DW

DW draws on the dusty, sample-heavy sound of Detroit beatmaking and the laid-back feel of West Coast hip-hop. Jazz loops, loose drums and off-kilter melodies sit at the heart of his sound, somewhere between a basement beat tape and a late-night session. Instrumental hip-hop with a mellow feel and a little roughness left in.
