Club Copy, explained

Short answers for press, collectors, and anyone searching for the Pacific Northwest label and record club.

What is Club Copy?

An independent Pacific Northwest record label based in British Columbia. House, jungle, instrumental hip-hop — digital and cassette, small editions — plus a record club for collecting those releases.

Is Club Copy a record club?

Yes — and a label. The imprint releases the music; the club is how you collect it. Free for alerts and first listens, Club ($5/yr) for 30% off all music, and Premium (from $10/yr) for 50% off all music plus Club Credit toward cassettes and limited pressings. Nothing ships unless you choose it.

Where is the label based?

Pacific Northwest · British Columbia, Canada.

Who’s on the roster?

Molly Blaze, Rosco, and L.T. Drifta.

Where do I buy releases?

In the library — digital and cassette. Club members get 30% off all music; Premium gets 50% off. Merch and physical editions ship to Canada and the US. Join for Free alerts or paid club levels.
