Club Copy, explained

What is Club Copy?

An independent Pacific Northwest record label based in British Columbia. House, jungle, instrumental hip-hop on digital and cassette, small editions, plus a record club for collecting those releases.

Is Club Copy a record club?

Yes, and a label. The imprint releases the music; the club is how you collect it. Free for alerts and first listens, Club ($5/yr) for 30% off all music, and Premium (from $10/yr) for 50% off all music plus Club Credit toward cassettes and limited pressings. Nothing ships unless you choose it.

Where is the label based?

Pacific Northwest · British Columbia, Canada.

Who is on the roster?

Forsberg, L.T. Drifta, Riscape.

Where do I buy releases?

In the library: digital and cassette. Club members get 30% off all music; Premium gets 50% off. Merch and physical editions ship to Canada and the US.
