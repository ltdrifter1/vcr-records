About

Music we want to keep.

Club Copy is electronic music from the Pacific Northwest. Small library. Long shelf life. Made to be listened to, held, and returned to.

The label

We release house, UK garage, and adjacent forms — records that feel finished when they leave, and still open when you play them years later.

Pressing, packaging, and merch are produced in limited batches. Release numbers (CC00x) mark each title in the library.

Founded by Charlie Archer. Published under Copy House Publishing.
