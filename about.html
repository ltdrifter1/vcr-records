About VCR

Independent electronic music with analog grit.

VCR Recordings is an independent label for underground electronic music — jungle pressure, hip-hop weight, cassette culture, and future-facing sound.

We release digital and limited physical formats with a focus on artwork, atmosphere, and music that rewards full listens. The site is built like a label gallery: light, textured, and design-forward — old-school VHS soul with a modern finish.

Browse the catalog, meet the artists, and support the music through merch and releases.
