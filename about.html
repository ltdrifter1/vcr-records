About

Music we want to keep.

Club Copy is an independent Pacific Northwest record label that runs a digital and physical record club. Small library. Long shelf life. Made to be listened to, held, and collected.

The label

We release house, UK garage, jungle, and adjacent forms — records that feel finished when they leave, and still open when you play them years later.

Pressing, packaging, and merch are produced in limited batches. Release numbers (CC00x) mark each title in the library. The record club is the invitation: a member number, digital releases at member price, and Club Credit toward physical editions — nothing ships unless you choose it.

Founded by Charlie Archer. Published under Copy House Publishing. Based in British Columbia, Canada.

Club Copy, explained

Short answers for press, collectors, and anyone searching for the Pacific Northwest label and record club.

What is Club Copy?

An independent Pacific Northwest record label based in British Columbia. House, jungle, and adjacent electronic music — digital and cassette, in small editions — plus a record club for collecting those releases.

Is Club Copy a record club?

Yes — and a label. The imprint releases the music; the club is how you collect it. Free for alerts and first listens, Club ($5/yr) for member digital pricing, and Premium (from $10/yr) with Club Credit toward cassettes and limited pressings. Nothing ships unless you choose it.

Where is the label based?

Pacific Northwest · British Columbia, Canada.

Who’s on the roster?

Inlet Knight and L.T. Drifta.

Where do I buy releases?

In the library — digital and cassette, with member pricing for Club members. Merch and physical editions ship to Canada and the US. Join for Free alerts or paid club levels.
