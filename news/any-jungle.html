Any Jungle

CJ Archer

Double-Edge has a new album coming. It’s called Any Jungle.

If you caught Enter, Double-Edge, you already know the shape of his thing — breaks that cut, bass that sits heavy, Pacific Northwest night air in the mix. Any Jungle keeps that pressure and opens the room a little. Same call sign. Wider map.

We’re not putting a street date on it yet. The record’s still finding its edges, and we’re happy to wait until it lands right. When it’s ready, you’ll hear it here first.

Until then, Enter is still the door. Press play, leave it alone for a few tracks, see where it takes you.

From Double-Edge:

“Aye man just press play. Most my records have a concept in there somewhere, but it’d be more interesting if you listen first and then tell me what you got from it.”
