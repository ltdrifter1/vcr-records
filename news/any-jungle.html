Any Jungle In?

CJ Archer

Double-Edge doesn’t want to explain it first.

Any Jungle is his next album. Same call sign as Enter, wider map. No street date yet. The record’s still finding its edges.

From Double-Edge:

“Aye man just press play. Most my records have a concept in there somewhere, but it’d be more interesting if you listen first and then tell me what you got from it.”

Until it lands, Enter is the door. Pre-order’s open.
