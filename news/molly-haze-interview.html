Molly Haze: A Short Conversation

Cumberland. Headphones up. The cover of this issue, in her own words.

posted by cj archer

Molly Haze lives in Cumberland, which is not a scene so much as a decision. House, tech house, UK garage — records that feel like a private temperature. We caught her the week Gorilla went up, kitchen light, one headphone in because the other ear was still on the rain.

k-i-s-s-i-n-g. That’s on your page like a dare. What does it mean when you’re actually making the record?

It means don’t perform the crush. Put it in the kick. I got tired of tracks that wink at you from the drop. I want the thing you play when she’s in the next room and you haven’t decided if you’re going to say it. Headphones up is not a caption. It’s how I mix. If it only works on a system the size of a car park, it isn’t finished.

Cumberland is a long way from the rooms these records imagine. Is that the point?

Nobody’s banging on the wall. You can let the last two minutes bloom. I like an island that doesn’t care if you “blew up.” The water does most of the talking. I send files from a town people only reach if they meant to, and somehow that’s when the house starts sounding like itself — not like an assignment from a city that already has enough DJs.

Gorilla is a Little Simz edit. DESIRE is yours. Still Riding is Barry Can't Swim. How do you know when an edit is a conversation and not a costume?

If I can hear her in it. Simz doesn’t need me. That’s why I did it — respect, not a grab. DESIRE is the one I couldn’t steal from anyone. The white label, the five minutes thirty-eight, the want you can wear. Still Riding is the ride home with the jacket on the passenger seat. Same week on purpose. I wanted both temperatures in your camera roll without anyone’s permission.

Who are you making this for?

The one who puts a record on and doesn’t announce it. The one who leaves the window cracked. The one who knows the difference between a night and a picture of a night. If you’re still in your coat in the kitchen, this is for you.

What should somebody play tonight?

Gorilla if they want to be looked at. DESIRE if they want to be alone on purpose. And if they want the room after that: Rosco’s Bridget In My Room. Door on the latch. She can stay.
