What I Want

MUNA put out the record. Silk chiffon, a gay bar, and a chorus that doesn’t whisper.

posted by cj archer

MUNA is out today on Phoebe’s Saddest Factory and “What I Want” is already the floor. Not the whispered floor. The one where you actually ask. Three women, synth-pop that remembers the 80s without asking a man to produce the memory. “Silk Chiffon” taught the timeline the pastel. This album is the night after: “Kind of Girl,” “Anything But Me,” the cowboy video, the language you use when you finally pick yourself.

The FADER will call it a queer pop landmark. Fine. In this province it’s the kitchen with the cheap disco bulb and everybody’s ex in the other room. They left the major. They kept the chorus. The underground that lives in gay bars already had the steps. The rest of the room can learn them tonight.

Play “What I Want” and don’t turn it down for the neighbours. If you start pointing at the ceiling before you remember you’re supposed to be cool about Pride month as a content window, that’s the review. She wants what she wants. Put it on. The chiffon was never the point. The ask was.
