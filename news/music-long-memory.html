Music Has a Long Memory

Music has always had a long memory.

CJ Archer

We forget a year. A break from 1973 will still walk into a room in Vancouver and move the furniture.

There is value in knowing where a feeling came from. The past is a language. It is not a costume. Remembering does not mean living in it. Music stays alive when people take what came before and do something a little wrong with it — their own nights, their own machines, their own weather.

Respect the roots. Leave the window open.
