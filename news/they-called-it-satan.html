Istanbul Pulled the Lights

Anyma’s ÆDEN date got revoked after the comments decided the visuals were satanic. Tickets were still for sale.

posted by cj archer

Istanbul pulled Anyma’s ÆDEN date after a pile of posts decided the lights were satanic. That’s the headline. Underneath it is older: a room full of people who came to be changed, and a city that got scared of the pictures.

DJ Mag had it first for the English-language desk: the governor’s office revoked permission for the September 12 show at Ataköy Marina Arena after clips from the tour went around framed as ritual. Same room that cancelled God Is An Astronaut hours before doors this year, same moral panic, different soundtrack. Tickets were still for sale while the official line was already no.

Anyma posted a few days later. The visuals are science-fiction, he said, darkness into light, five years of designers and animators pulling from art history — not a religion, not a culture, and none of it AI. The part that stuck: he says the campaign ran his clips through AI until they looked like whatever the comment section needed them to be, then handed that version to the city. No warning. No meeting. Permit gone.

The underground has always had a problem with looking too much like itself on camera. Now the camera lies on purpose and a city takes it at face value. A light show that didn’t get to finish the story. That’s the whole item.
