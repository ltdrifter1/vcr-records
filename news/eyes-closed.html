Eyes Closed

There’s a face people make when a track finally lands — eyes shut, jaw loose, no performance for the room. I’ve seen it on dancefloors, sure. I notice it more on buses, in kitchens, anywhere someone thinks nobody’s watching.

That’s the listening I’m interested in. Not the pose. Not the caption. Just the private second where the music is doing something and you’re honest enough to let it show on your face.

A lot of Club Copy ends up aimed at that moment. Double-Edge when the breaks get heavy enough that you stop talking. Inlet Knight when a loop keeps circling and you realize you’ve been standing still for three minutes. Different records. Same small tell.

If you need one of those today, put something on and close your eyes for a minute. The room will still be there when you open them.
