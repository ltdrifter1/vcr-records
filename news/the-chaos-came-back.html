MAYHEM

Gaga, fourteen tracks, Gesaffelstein in the credits. Abracadabra has been living in kitchens since the Grammys.

posted by cj archer

Lady Gaga put MAYHEM out today. Fourteen tracks. Shangri-La. Andrew Watt, Cirkut, Gesaffelstein on “Killah.” “Abracadabra” has been doing the rounds since the Grammys break — Siouxsie in the DNA, acid techno with a chorus that wants a floor and isn’t sorry about it. “Disease” was the October warning. This is the weather.

Everybody wants to call it a return to the Fame Monster girl. That’s a fan-forum sentence. She never left the industrial. She just spent a few years in other rooms. If you learned to dance in a leather jacket in 2009, your body already filed this. I played “Abracadabra” in a kitchen here with the window cracked and started the spell before the drop, which is embarrassing and also the point.

Gesaffelstein in the credits is not a souvenir for boys who collect techno they don’t play. It’s a woman who still wants the strobe to mean something. Copacabana will get the numbers. Coachella will get the remix. Cumberland gets the record. Put it on.
