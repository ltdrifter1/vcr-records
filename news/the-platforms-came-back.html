The Platforms Came Back

Rooms opened for real. Buffalo, a bathroom compact, and a heel that remembered 1999 before Vogue did.

posted by cj archer · Apr 8, 2022

April 2022 and the rooms are actually rooms again. The girls showed up in the height they practiced on linoleum. Buffalo. A boot that could take a wet pavement. A going-out top that finally left the chair. Nobody waited for a trend report to say Y2K. They had the compact. They had the stall. They had eighteen months of doing the mouth for a webcam that wasn’t even a club. The floor is the photograph. The bathroom is the edit.

The boys will call it a revival. It isn’t. It’s a return. 1999 the platforms were how you saw over the booth. 2022 they’re how you clock that the cloakroom exists again. Cute as armor did not clock in this spring. It never clocked out. The petition about pajama pants is years away. The stall already decided: if she can dance in it, it’s a policy.

Hold her bag. Don’t film the stall. If the heel is impractical, that’s the point — the night is impractical. That’s why you left the apartment. The xerox of the evening is already on the collarbone. The sidewalk can complain in the morning.
