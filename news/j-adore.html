j'adore

Two tracks from Rosco. Windows cracked, city going violet, a passenger you haven’t figured out yet.

A car ride that doesn’t end when the song does. You know the one — windows cracked, city going violet, somebody in the passenger seat you haven’t figured out yet.

Two tracks from Rosco: “don’t cry” and “j'adore.” Soft edges, late light, a fisheye blue car that looks like it still smells like the heater. We’ve had the second one on repeat the way you replay a text you already answered. House, but the private kind. The kind you put on when you’re pretending you’re only driving to the store.

If you want a short way into Rosco, start here. If you want the whole weather system, the self-titled is waiting. Leave this one on until the streetlights look expensive.
