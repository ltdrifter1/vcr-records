Scene Report

  1. Radial Wants the Midsize Room Tottenham’s new 250-cap listening club opens out of Ten87 Studios with a Whitaker-tuned Martin Audio/Funktion-One rig, a no-phones floor, and an opening weekend that stacks jungle, UK bass, house and garage. The gossip is the sound. The story is the room. 17 Oct 2026
  2. Eight Rooms, One Basement From 2008 to 2012 the Guvernment was eighth in the world. Toronto’s other night was a basement under the Silver Dollar, and a tent in a park. 17 Oct 2026
  3. No Fun City Still Raids the Rooms Vancouver sells vibrant nightlife to the visitor brochure, then inspects the rooms that actually dance. 12 Sep 2026
  4. Madame Lou's Is Dead. Bottlerocket Wants the Basement. Two Crocodile rooms gone, a hundred jobs cut, a comedy sale, and a March 2027 dance floor that is not yours yet. 12 Sep 2026
  5. Portland Finally Got an All-Ages Temple The Dancin' Bare is a Friends of Noise room now: no liquor, four hundred caps, and the bloodline showed up with a drum kit. 12 Sep 2026
  6. Party Like the Lease Is Ending Cobalt came back as punk, metal, hardcore, then a dance floor till two, with a hotel clock in the coverage and a fire that was only a blip. 12 Sep 2026
  7. FIFA vs the Underground Granville posted a record week and the warehouses posted shutdowns: two nightlives, same city, same winter, and no, that is not the same as a conspiracy. 12 Sep 2026

Review

  1. The Missing P Sebadoh's Harmacy turns thirty on November 20 with sixteen extra tracks and a zine drawn by Lou Barlow, who has called the record a heartbreaker. A band that began on home-made cassettes got a Sub Pop budget, a studio and, by Barlow's account, a drummer problem. What is an album worth to the person who made it? 10 Oct 2026
  2. Crystal Tears on the Westside Six years after Chilombo, Jhené Aiko finishes Westside Whimsy the way she started: mixtape stubbornness, Fisticuffs in the booth, and a love letter to a coast that burned. 12 Sep 2026
  3. Love Deluxe Sade's fourth album, Epic, 26 October 1992. Quiet, on this record, is a decision with a rhythm section under it. 4 Sep 2026
