DESIRE

Molly Haze, original mix, 5:38. Headphones up. Same day as Still Riding.

molly haze5:38house

~ turn my headphones up ~

That’s the instruction, and Molly Haze means it. DESIRE is five minutes thirty-eight of original mix — white promo label on black, the kind of house that doesn’t blink first. I played it walking home with one bud in, the other ear on the street, and it still felt like the private version. Not a peak-time stunt. A want you can wear.

Same day the Barry Can't Swim edit went up, Still Riding. Two temperatures, one desk. DESIRE is the room with the lights still ambitious. The edit is the ride home, jacket on the passenger seat, city going blue at the edges. If you’ve been waiting for Molly to sound like Molly instead of like an assignment, this is the week.

Cumberland is a long way from the rooms these records imagine, which is probably why they work. Nobody in the next apartment is going to bang on the wall. You can let the last two minutes bloom.
