The Art of Sound and Zen

Sit with a loop until it stops asking.

CJ Archer

Sit with a loop until it stops asking. Phone in the other room. No skip. Notice when your mind leaves — the text you didn’t send, the outfit you already decided against — and come back without making it a project.

The record doesn’t need you to be good at this. It needs you to stay. Some nights what’s left is a kick drum. Some nights it’s you.
