Labubu on the Strap

Lisa clipped a gremlin to a Louis. Dua doubled up. The reseller tabs went ugly. The floor did not care.

posted by cj archer

Lisa clipped a toothy elf to a Louis Vuitton and the rest of the year happened. Labubu — Kasing Lung’s little monster, Pop Mart, blind box — hanging off Birkins in Paris and tote bags in this province. Dua Lipa doubled up in June. Rihanna. Gaga. The reseller tabs went ugly. Nobody on the floor asked for your StockX screenshot.

Same argument as the pajama pants, just with sharper teeth. Cute as armor. A charm as a tell. The boys who think a rave is a uniform called it childish. The girls who used to safety-pin a pacifier to a backpack already knew the lineage. You don’t owe the strobe a silent bag because somebody’s older cousin thinks folklore elves are for children.

Paris Fashion Week wore them like a password. Glasgow is already printing cats on flyers. Somewhere between the two, a twenty-seven-year-old in this climate is walking into a kitchen with a gremlin on the strap and the good boots. She is not the problem. Wear the monster. The door cannot tax a charm.
