The Kitchen Was the Club

No door. No cloakroom. Somebody sent a link. You lit something and you stayed on mute.

posted by cj archer

Before anybody touches the speaker, somebody lights something. Then somebody pastes a Zoom. Then somebody is DJing from a bedroom in another province and you are in socks on the linoleum like that’s a floor. April 2021. The rooms are still a brochure. The kitchen is the club. The ritual is the whole night: kettle, stick, link, mute yourself unless you’re the one with the USB.

1999 this would have been a mixtape on the counter and a girl doing her eyes in the toaster. 2021 the mixtape is a Discord booth and the toaster still works. You do not clap on the two. You do not talk over the drop. You send a heart in the chat and you mean it. The afters are the same apartment because there is nowhere else. That’s not a punchline. That’s the liturgy.

When they open the real door later this year, some of you will pretend this never counted. It counted. The body learned the night without a PA. If you still put incense on before the aux, that’s not nostalgia. That’s the practice you built when the only room you could trust was yours.
