Baddest of Them All

Eliza Rose took garage to the chart. The booth already knew the chant.

posted by cj archer

It’s Friday and B.O.T.A. is in the Top 10 like it still belongs to the booth that made it. Eliza Rose and Interplanetary Criminal. Mixmag Lab in June, Glastonbury, a girl walking out of the stone circle yelling I made this. Lisa Lisa in the sample. Korg M1 in the spine. Speed garage that didn’t ask the chart if it was allowed to have a woman’s mouth on it.

The underground spent a year watching UKG get revived by boys with the right Discord. Then a London girl wrote a chant and the whole island started shouting baddest of them all like it was 1999 and also tomorrow. Peach played it. Shanti Celeste played it. The field at Love International ate it. Now the radio is pretending it discovered the organ.

The gossip isn’t that garage “went pop.” Garage has been pop every summer somebody let a woman on the mic. The gossip is that the song still works at 2am in a room that doesn’t care about the Official Chart. Play it on Island Highway. If you start the chant before the drop and you are not performing a bit, that’s the review. Let it go number one. The floor had it first.
