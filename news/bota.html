Baddest of Them All

Eliza Rose took garage to the chart. The booth already knew the chant.

posted by cj archer

Eliza Rose and Interplanetary Criminal’s “B.O.T.A.” is in the Top 10 like it still belongs to the booth that made it. Mixmag Lab in June, Glastonbury, a girl walking out of the stone circle yelling I made this. Lisa Lisa in the sample. Korg M1 in the spine.

UKG spent a year getting revived by boys with the right Discord. Then a London girl wrote a chant and the island started shouting baddest of them all like it was 1999 and also tomorrow. Peach played it. Shanti Celeste played it. The radio is late, which is how you know it was real.
