Inlet Knight

Rosco’s first full statement. Eleven tracks on cassette. A boat on actual water.

Eleven tracks cut for late nights and the kind of drive where you miss your exit on purpose.

Rosco’s self-titled, Inlet Knight, is the first full statement — written and performed by him, produced by CJ Archer, dubbed to a limited cassette because some records still want to be held. The artwork is a boat on actual water, not a moodboard of water. Vancouver does that. You can live here a decade and still not be done looking at it.

Start at the top and leave it alone. If you need a single door in, j'adore will walk you there in a car. This one is the whole crossing.
