Six Times

On Tuesday 6 October, at [UNVRS] in Ibiza, DJ Mag crowned David Guetta the world’s No. 1 DJ for a sixth time. Anyma moved to 3. Charlotte de Witte to 6. John Summit, Mau P and Chris Stassy each climbed 22 places. The top two names did not move.

posted by cj archer · Oct 8, 2026

The name at the top stayed where it was. David Guetta finished first in DJ Mag’s Top 100 DJs for the sixth time, read out live at [UNVRS] on Tuesday night, the second year the reveal has been staged in that room. The first win was 2011. The others were 2020, 2021, 2023 and 2025. Martin Garrix stays at 2. Armin van Buuren, five titles of his own and the trance prize again, is at 7. Guetta is now alone ahead of both of them.

DJ Mag says a record number of votes came in from 234 territories. The largest European piles were the UK, France, Spain, Italy, Belgium, the Netherlands and Portugal. The USA and China were among the biggest blocs, and Chinese voters used a dedicated app. Svalbard had a say. Wallis and Futuna had a say. A public ballot that wide counts the name the most people can already pronounce.

A summer without the single

In the interview published with the result, Guetta called it the first summer in a long run without a pop hit, and still the biggest summer he has had. Galactic Circus, his residency at [UNVRS], and Fuck Me I’m Famous at Ushuaïa sold out faster than before. Three nights at the Stade de France moved more than 240,000 tickets, with Armin van Buuren, Afrojack, Black Coffee, Jennifer Lopez, Akon, Bebe Rexha and the Black Eyed Peas among the guests. He told the magazine that everything can be faked, and that selling tickets cannot. The FIFA anthem, “DNA (More Than A Game),” put him with Andrea Bocelli, Megan Thee Stallion and EJAE. “Awake Tonight” put him back with Afrojack and Sia. The poll has a long memory for a chorus, and a longer one for a stadium that actually filled.

He also kept a foot in the club the magazine could point at. Future Rave with MORTEN. A first back-to-back with Carl Cox during Galactic Circus. A forthcoming record, “Caught Up,” with Marlon Hoffstadt, who himself moved from 100 to 97. The Monolith show has been to Red Rocks and Coachella. In summer 2027 it is booked for the O2 in London, the room’s 20th year. None of that changes what the top of a public list is for. It explains why the top did not move.

Twenty-two places, three times

The movement is under him. Anyma climbed seven places, from 10 to 3. Charlotte de Witte went from 9 to 6 and kept the techno title. FISHER slipped from 7 to 9 and took the house title again, after another season in the same building. Vintage Culture is the new face in the top 10, up one place to 10. Afrojack dropped seven, to 15, and left the ten.

House did the climbing the magazine chose to medal. For the first time the highest-climber prize is shared, three ways, 22 places each. John Summit went from 46 to 24. Mau P went from 77 to 55. Chris Stassy, billed until now as Chris Stussy, went from 81 to 59. Fred again.. gained 17 and landed at 16. The Martinez Brothers gained 15 and landed at 28. Michael Bibi gained 11, to 37. Josh Baker, who holds a residency at Amnesia, is the highest new entry, straight in at 40. Mahmut Orhan, from Bursa, is the next debut, at 42. Padre Guilherme, a Catholic priest whose sets DJ Mag calls the most surprising new entry on the list, is at 79.

Indira Paganotto rose ten places, broke the top 30 at 26, and took the first psy prize the poll has given. Two nights later she was back in the [UNVRS] main room closing ARTCORE. That week on the island is the other piece.

The other end of the hundred

The published list, as set down once the countdown finished, thins out where a smaller club would start. Honey Dijon finished 98th, down from 97. KI/KI, a Dutch acid and trance name, closes the hundred as a new entry. I Hate Models, in at 79 last year, is off it, listed at 106 in the next fifty. Chris Lake, inside the hundred a year ago, is listed at 108. Joseph Capriati debuted at 68. Sonny Fodera at 80. Shimza at 75. Prospa and Cloonee, whose “Free Your Mind” was the house record of the year that would not leave a playlist, both made the hundred for the first time. The ballot can arrive late. It still arrives with a chorus attached.

The room they read the names in had already won a poll of its own. In April, DJ Mag’s Top 100 Clubs put [UNVRS] at No. 1 in its first year. It opened on 30 May 2025 on the old Privilege site, a 10,000-capacity build from The Night League, and the magazine said it was the first club to go straight to the top since the staff-made list of 2006, and the first to do it inside a year of opening. On 6 October the DJ ballot was counted in that room. Lost Frequencies, Charlotte de Witte, Anyma, Korolova, Anastazja and a GORDO back-to-back with Vintage Culture played before Guetta’s two hours. Anastazja, Chicago-born and a Rinse FM resident, took the future-star prize on the same night. Luciano took outstanding contribution. The building and the winner were elected by the same kind of vote. One of them has been No. 1 for fifteen years of restarts. The other has been open for one summer and a closing.

Bridges for Music sits beside the result, which is how the magazine frames the count. Last year 42 names from the list put in £170,832, for the academy in Langa. This year each artist in the hundred is invited to sponsor one student there. The invitation is not a placing. The placing is still a show of hands, and the hands, from 234 territories, put the same man first.
