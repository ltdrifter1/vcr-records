The Bathroom Line

The floor is for the photograph. You go to the stall to finish the night.

posted by cj archer

You go in with the night still on your mouth and come out with a policy. Compact. Tissue. The girl who holds the stall door with her boot so it doesn’t swing. Somebody’s charger. Somebody’s gum. The light is criminal and that’s useful — you see what the strobe was hiding.

1999 it was Body Shop glitter gel in a tube you stole from a roommate and a MAC that never got returned. 2003 it was lip gloss in the pocket of low-rise everything, the camera phone still a rumour. This year the stall is the same square, the flashlight is better, the compact is holographic. Cute as armor did not start on TikTok. The bathroom line is older than the booth.

Promoters talk about the room like it belongs to the PA. The girls know the room with the lock. That’s where the fit gets finished. That’s where you decide if you’re staying. That’s where somebody cries for four minutes and then asks who has blotting papers. No DJ has ever improved a night from in here. That’s why it works.

A petition can have opinions about pajama pants. It cannot touch a stall. If she redraws the lip between tracks, the night just got a second intro. Hold her bag. Don’t film it. The xerox of the evening is already on her collarbone.
