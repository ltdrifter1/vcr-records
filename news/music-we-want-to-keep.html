Music We Want to Keep

By CJ Archer

There’s a moment — usually after midnight — when a record stops being something you check out and becomes a place you stand inside. That’s the music we’re here for.

Club Copy is a small Pacific Northwest label. House, jungle, adjacent forms. We only put out what we’d still want to hear in ten years.

Double-Edge and Inlet Knight aren’t a “roster” in the industry sense. They’re two frequencies we trust — breaks with pressure, soft rooms with patience. Same coast. Same refusal to fake it.

Keep what keeps you. Press play when the house is quiet.
