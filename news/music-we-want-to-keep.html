Music We Want to Keep

I collect records the way some people collect stories — unevenly, with gaps, and with a soft spot for the ones that still sound good after the room empties. Club Copy came out of that habit more than a business plan.

We put out house and jungle from the Pacific Northwest, mostly from people we already trusted. Double-Edge. Inlet Knight. Small editions. Digital when it makes sense. Cassette when it feels right. The idea was never to flood anything. Just to keep a short shelf of music that holds up on a second year, not just a second week.

If you’ve been looking for a way into the label, start with the library. If you want faces with the names, the artists page is there. Either way — press play, leave it on, see what sticks.
