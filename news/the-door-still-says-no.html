The Door Still Says No

Berghain sold you a ticket. The door can still refund it.

posted by cj archer

Berghain is selling tickets. DJ Mag dropped them to 21. An Ibiza hyperclub with one season on the clock took number one. A poll like that measures voting, capacity, marketing. The queue on Rüdersdorfer Straße measures none of it.

Position 21 changes nothing about who gets sent home. Groove Store’s quieter story this year is the same as the door’s: you can buy a ticket and still not be in.

If you have ever stood in a line that was also a story you told yourself about who you are, you already know how this ends. The ranking is a magazine. The no is still a person.
