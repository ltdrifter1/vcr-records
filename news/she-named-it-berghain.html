She's Never Been to Berghain

Rosalía, Björk, Yves Tumor, the LSO, German in the first breath, and then she said she’s never been inside.

Rosalía dropped “Berghain” today with Björk, Yves Tumor, and the London Symphony Orchestra, German in the first breath. It is the lead single from LUX, out November 7, and then she said she’s never been inside. The name is a threshold, not a guest list. Hildegard of Bingen is in the liner notes. The comments wanted a nightclub and she sent a cathedral.

This desk already knows the actual door. The queue on Rüdersdorfer Straße does not care that a pop star borrowed the word, which is the gossip and also the compliment: she treated the club as a nervous system, not a font. Seine Angst ist meine Angst. His fear is my fear. Play it once through without Googling the guest list. If your shoulders drop before Björk arrives, you didn’t need the door.

People who dance in this climate never needed Berghain to understand a locked room. Motomami was the body. This is the afters: the kitchen light, the hymn, the part where you stop performing irony. She named a state, and most of us were already in it.
