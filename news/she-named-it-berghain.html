She's Never Been to Berghain

Rosalía, Björk, Yves Tumor, the LSO. German in the first breath. Then she said she’s never been inside.

posted by cj archer

Rosalía dropped “Berghain” today — Björk, Yves Tumor, the London Symphony Orchestra, German in the first breath. Lead single from LUX, November 7. Then she said she’s never been inside. The name is a threshold, not a guest list. Hildegard of Bingen in the liner notes. The comments wanted a nightclub. She sent a cathedral.

This desk already knows the actual door. The queue on Rüdersdorfer Straße does not care that a pop star borrowed the word. That’s the gossip, and also the compliment: she treated the club as a nervous system, not a font. Seine Angst ist meine Angst. His fear is my fear. Play it once through without Googling the guest list. If your shoulders drop before Björk arrives, you didn’t need the door.

The girls who dance in this climate never needed to get into Berghain to understand a locked room. Motomami was the body. This is the afters — the kitchen light, the hymn, the part where you stop performing irony. She named a state. Most of us were already in it.
