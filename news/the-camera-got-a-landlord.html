Boiler Room Got Sold

DICE sold the webcam to Superstruct. The rooms are asking who owns the rectangle.

posted by cj archer

DICE sold Boiler Room today to Superstruct Entertainment — the European live giant KKR bought last summer for a reported $1.4 billion. Undisclosed sum. Blaise Bellville’s webcam, the one that made a London basement feel like a passport, now reports to a festival conglomerate. DICE keeps the tickets. Superstruct keeps the brand. The rooms keep the night, if they still want it.

A billion views. A hundred and twenty markets. That grainy rectangle turned a DJ into a rumour you forwarded at 1am. You could be in Peckham and also on a wet pavement in Cumberland, watching the same drop, performing the same yes. Platforms get sold. That’s not gossip. What’s gossip is that the underground spent fifteen years letting a camera stand in for a door.

Some lineups will keep saying yes. Some already started saying no. Put on the last stream you actually remember — not the one you screenshotted — and ask whether you were in the room or just watching someone else be in it. The night never lived in the rectangle. The rectangle just got a new letterhead.
