Sun & Moon (Eclipse Parts 1-3)

There’s a Japanese legend that says the sun and moon once loved each other, but because of time and distance, they were never meant to meet.

So the universe created the eclipse.

And maybe that’s why eclipses feel so powerful and emotional: a rare moment when the sun and moon could finally meet, even if only for a little while.

They remind us there’s no such thing as impossible love. Because one day, even the most distant hearts meet only once. And maybe that is why some moments in love feel so painful. Not because it was never real, but because timing was never kind to it.

Some people enter your life like bright sunlight. Some stay in your heart like the moon: quiet, distant, impossible to forget.

You may not always get to keep the person you love. But sometimes the universe gives you one moment, one look, one memory, one meeting. Just to remind you that what you felt was not an illusion.

Inlet Knight’s Eclipse run is three singles built around that idea: Sun & Moon, Solar, Lunar. Start with Part 1 and let them meet in order.
