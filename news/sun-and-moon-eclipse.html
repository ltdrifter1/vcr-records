Sun & Moon (Eclipse Parts 1–3)

Inlet Knight’s Eclipse series is out on Club Copy. Three house singles under one catalogue number — CC012 — released as digital downloads at $1.99 CAD each. The arc runs Sun & Moon (Eclipse Part 1), Solar (Eclipse Part 2), then Lunar (Eclipse Part 3).

House from Cumberland, BC. Locked drums, long loops, the kind of records that stay on after the room clears. Produced by CJ Archer for Club Copy and Copy House Publishing. If you’ve been following Inlet Knight through the self-titled album, Together, and j'adore, this is the next run — shorter entries, same honesty.

The three parts

Sun & Moon (Eclipse Part 1) opens the series. Somewhere between the two — the first light in the set. Start here if you want the through-line clean.

Solar (Eclipse Part 2) follows. Daylight heat, still house, still Inlet Knight. Same catalogue family as Part 1; a different angle on the night.

Lunar (Eclipse Part 3) closes the eclipse. Late side. Pull it after Solar and the sequence holds — sun, solar, lunar.

How to listen

Each single is digital only: streaming previews on the release pages, full download after checkout. No shipping. Grab one part or collect all three from the library or shop. More from Inlet Knight — including cassette and digital catalogue — lives on the artist page.
