The New Sound of the Greek Underground

Ioannis Rigas grew up on piano in Athens. The clubs came later. This is him now.

posted by cj archer · Artist

Ioannis Rigas records as Riscape. He grew up in Athens, on classical piano, at the National Conservatory of Chalandri — hours in a room learning to wait for a note. The clubs came after. Production at SAE and Grey Studios. You can still hear the training: not as a flex, as patience. The records sound like 6am, when the room has thinned out and whoever is left is not performing for anybody.

He started putting music out in 2020. Mystery Freedom Records, out of the Netherlands, picked him up in 2021. Then the melodic club EPs — Unity, Two x S — the kind of records that want a big system and still work in a kitchen. This year he hit Beatport No. 1 on the Trance (Main Floor) Top 100 New Releases chart with his remix of “Synth Magic” by Alphed Le Cornett & JARR Head. Away from the studio he hosts Riscape Reality Radio. Spinnin’, Sirup, EDM Joy have been on it. That’s not a pitch. That’s a person who already had a life before we showed up.

This is the new sound of the Greek underground, which is just a way of saying: house from Athens that doesn’t ask you to pretend you’re on holiday. I put one on walking to the corner store and came home empty-handed. We signed him because the tracks stay in the body after the night is over. More soon — starting with the cassette.
