House Music Outlasts the Room

posted by cj archer04 aug 26

Riscape is the hook you chew in the cloakroom queue — still on it when security start the shuffle and the night bus is doing the afters.

House music did not invent staying out. It made staying out the whole argument. The kick is a policy. The bassline is why nobody sits even when the calves are bargaining. You do not remember the running order. You remember a shirt glued to a stranger’s back and the fact that three hours later you were still in the weather of it.

That is the impact, if we are being honest. The sweaty club is not a side effect. It is the instrument. Condensation on the pipes. A floor that gives a little. A room that has already decided the night is not finished. Security start the shuffle because the clock says otherwise, and the floor has not agreed. Then the cloakroom, which is a second dancefloor with worse lighting, and then the night bus, which is a third — fluorescent, ugly, still humming.

Riscape writes for all three. Athens, not the villa postcard. A piano kid who learned that a rushed bar is a personal insult, then walked into a room with no interval applause and a kick already moving. The concert-hall posture did not make it home. What made it home is the phrase: a hook that refuses to clock out when the engineer kills the smoke.

Most of this year’s peak pile is a riser, a drop, a lighting cue. House, when it is working, stays in the teeth. You ruin a taxi with it. You put it on in a kitchen at five because sitting down would be a kind of surrender. From 2020 he has been shipping that kind of record — peak time minus the laser sermon — and I have been leaning on it at a volume the stairwell has opinions about.

The Dutch heard him first, as they tend to. Mystery Freedom. Unity. Two x S. A remix of Alphed Le Cornett and JARR Head’s “Synth Magic” that sat at Beatport No. 1 on the Trance (Main Floor) Top 100 New Releases chart, a line that will get him dates and still fail to explain why a room full of wet cotton keeps winning. Off the desk he runs Riscape Reality Radio: whatever currently has him pacing. Fine. The CV is not why you are still moving.

The bit that counts is older than any chart. House taught a city how to stay. It made late the point. Riscape plays like somebody who got told off for jumping ahead: when the kick lands, the left hand is still on the bass, and the hook is still in your mouth on the way home.
