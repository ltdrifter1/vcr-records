Welcome, Riscape

Athens house. Classical piano. The new sound of the Greek underground — now on Club Copy.

ArtistAug 4, 2026

posted by cj archer

Club Copy is pleased to welcome Riscape to the roster.

Riscape is the recording name of Ioannis Rigas, a producer and DJ based in Athens, Greece. He began on classical piano — National Conservatory of Chalandri — before moving into clubs and electronic production at SAE and Grey Studios. The records that followed keep that training in the room: melody first, peak-time instinct second, nothing cheap in the lift.

He started releasing in 2020. In 2021 he joined Dutch imprint Mystery Freedom Records and built a catalog of melodic club EPs, including Unity and Two x S. This year he reached Beatport No. 1 on the Trance (Main Floor) Top 100 New Releases chart with his remix of “Synth Magic” by Alphed Le Cornett & JARR Head. Away from the studio he hosts Riscape Reality Radio, a mix series for the records that shape his own. His music has found support from Spinnin’ Records, Sirup Music, and EDM Joy, with airplay and DJ backing across the international house circuit.

We signed him because the tracks stay in the body after the night is over. House from Athens — not the postcard. The rooms that empty at 7am. He is in. More soon.
