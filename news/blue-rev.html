Easy on Your Own

Alvvays came back. Blue Rev, a Canadian cooler, and the night drive that doesn’t end.

posted by cj archer

Blue Rev is out today and the Canadian girls already had “Pharmacist” in the car since July. Toronto band. Polyvinyl. A record named after a cooler you drank in a park when you were nineteen. Five years after Antisocialites. Flooded basement, lost demos, a border that wouldn’t let them rehearse. Then they sat down in Los Angeles and played the whole thing like a dare. Pitchfork will stamp Best New Music. In this province it’s the fluorescent of a 24-hour pharmacy through the windshield and Molly Rankin’s voice doing the thing it does to a winter.

“Easy On Your Own?” is the one. “Belinda Says.” “Very Online Guy.” Shoegaze that still has a chorus. The underground that lives in the ghost of Tiny Mix Tapes and The Quietus already queued it. The rest of the timeline will call it indie. Fine. It’s the record you put on when the rain starts on the Lions Gate and you don’t want to talk yet.

Play “Easy On Your Own?” and don’t skip when the guitars go white. If you start singing in the voice you use when nobody’s in the passenger seat, that’s the review. Named after a drink. Still goes down. The pharmacist is closed. The song isn’t.
