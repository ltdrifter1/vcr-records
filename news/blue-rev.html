Easy on Your Own

Alvvays came back. Blue Rev, a Canadian cooler, and the night drive that doesn’t end.

posted by cj archer

Blue Rev is out and “Pharmacist” has been in Canadian cars since July. Toronto. Polyvinyl. A record named after a cooler you drank in a park when you were nineteen. Five years after Antisocialites: flooded basement, lost demos, a border that wouldn’t let them rehearse. Then they sat down in Los Angeles and played the whole thing like a dare.

“Easy On Your Own?” is the one. “Belinda Says.” “Very Online Guy.” Shoegaze that still has a chorus. In this province it’s the fluorescent of a 24-hour pharmacy through the windshield, Molly Rankin’s voice doing the thing it does to a winter.
