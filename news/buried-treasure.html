Buried Treasure

Club Copy

You know the feeling. Standing over a bin with wet shoes and no list. Flip. Skip. Flip again. Most of it leaves your hands. Then one side stops you — not because it’s rare, because it still moves.

That’s the dig. Not a flex. Not a hunt for the most expensive sticker. Just patience with plastic and cardboard until something earns a trip home. Some people dig for the night ahead. Some dig for a sample. Some dig so they don’t forget where a night went. Same motion. Different reasons.

Club Copy comes out of that habit. A room, a hard drive, a picky ear. Records that hold when the feed moves on. If you wouldn’t keep it as a listener, it doesn’t ship.

Buried treasure isn’t locked in a vault. It’s whatever survives the sort — the break that still hits in the rain, the loop you play twice, the sleeve you don’t put back. Pull it up. Press play. See who stays.
