Buried Treasure

Flip until something holds.

Club Copy

Flip until something holds. That’s the whole job, and it will make you look unserious to anyone who thinks a label is a brand first.

Wet shoes, no list, bin after bin. Most of it leaves your hands. Then one side stops you — not because it’s rare, because it still moves, because you’d play it for someone you were trying not to impress. The ride in the photo could be anywhere that still lets you hunt. That’s the point.

Club Copy comes out of that habit. If you wouldn’t keep it as a listener, it doesn’t ship. The crate was never supposed to be a personality. It was supposed to be a feeling you couldn’t shake on the walk home.
