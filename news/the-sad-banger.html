The Desert Played the Sad Banger

Four Tet, Fred again.., Skrillex. Coachella. The underground borrowed a desert.

posted by cj archer · Apr 18, 2023

Cumberland did not buy a Coachella ticket. Cumberland watched. Weekend One: three names that used to mean three different rooms, one sunset. Four Tet’s patience. Fred’s open wound. Skrillex after the bro years — still loud, finally listening.

The clips hit the group chat and for once nobody performed having been there. Actual Life already taught a certain kitchen that a festival could sound like a voice note. The desert just gave it a livestream.

It is not a secret rave. It is what happens when the secret gets a sandlot.
