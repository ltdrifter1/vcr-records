You Are (Love)

Riscape. First Club Copy cassette. Out September 18.

ReleaseSep 8, 2026CC004

posted by earl tie

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — Cumberland / Athens — Club Copy will release You Are (Love), the debut Club Copy cassette from Athens house producer Riscape, on September 18, 2026.

The EP is four tracks — You Are (Love), Make it Right, Action, Days Like This — written and produced by Ioannis Rigas. Cassette only. Pre-order is open now. Record Club members will be notified first; tapes ship on street date.

Riscape joined the label in August. You Are (Love) is the first statement. Catalog number CC004.
