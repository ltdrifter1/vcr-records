You Are (Love)

Riscape’s first Club Copy cassette. Four tracks. September 18. Pre-order if you want one you can actually flip.

posted by cj archer

I have been playing the piano parts in the kitchen with the windows open, which is a ridiculous thing to admit about a house record, and also the reason we wanted him. Riscape’s first tape for us lands September 18. You Are (Love) — four tracks, cassette only. You can still hear the conservatory in it. Not as homework. As blood.

The cuts are You Are (Love), Make it Right, Action, Days Like This. Written and produced by Ioannis Rigas. Pre-order is open. Record Club hears first; tapes ship on street date. Catalog number CC004. I keep putting “Days Like This” on when I’m doing my face, which is not when I thought I’d be living with an Athens record, and here we are.

We signed him in August. This is the first statement. If you have been waiting for those late rooms to live in a box you can hold on the bus home, this is that box.
