You Are (Love)

Riscape. Athens house. Cassette. Out September 18.

ReleaseSep 18, 2026CC004

posted by cj archer

A title that doesn’t beg. It just tells you.

You Are (Love) is Riscape’s first Club Copy cassette. Four tracks: You Are (Love), Make it Right, Action, Days Like This. House from Athens — not the postcard, the rooms that empty at 7am. Written and produced by Ioannis Rigas. Classical piano first, Conservatory then SAE, then the clubs, then records that stay in the body after the night is over. This is the first one with us.

We’ve been sitting with it the way you sit with a track that already knows the room. Melodic without going soft. Peak-time without the cheap lift. The kind of record you put on when the system is still loud and nobody has asked what time it is. He calls the series Riscape Reality Radio. This one doesn’t need a mix to make the point.

September 18. Join the Record Club if you want it first — we’ll send it when it’s ready.
