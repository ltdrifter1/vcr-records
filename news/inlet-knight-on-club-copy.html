Welcome, Inlet Knight

Inlet Knight is on Club Copy. He makes house in Cumberland, British Columbia — patient drums, soft loops, music that holds up past a single play.

The self-titled debut is out on cassette and digital, with the Together EP alongside it. New today: j'adore, a two-track single. If you want a shorter way in, start there — or with Letters From Another Era.
