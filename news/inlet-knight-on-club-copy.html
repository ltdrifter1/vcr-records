Inlet Knight on Club Copy

Inlet Knight makes house music in Cumberland, British Columbia — patient drums, soft loops, and melodies that tend to stick around after the track ends. Unhurried late-night music, built to hold up past a single play.

His self-titled debut is available on cassette and digital, alongside the Together EP. Letters From Another Era, a four-track digital EP, is a good shorter entry point if you’re new to his work.
