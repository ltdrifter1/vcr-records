We Signed Forsberg

Forsberg’s Bandcamp bio is one line, house from Cumberland, and the music page now holds twelve titles.

Feature

Oct 22, 2024
author
CJ Archer

Cumberland

Forsberg’s Bandcamp bio is one sentence. “House music from Cumberland, BC.” The location line names the same town: Cumberland, British Columbia. On j’adore, the earliest release still on the music page, the tags are deep house, electronic, uk garage, club, lo-fi house, and speed garage. That is the self-portrait. The discography is now long enough to read beside it.

j’adore came out on 10 August 2026. Two tracks: don’t cry, and j’adore. Pitch Black came out on 18 October 2026, three tracks: Neighbourhood Spy, Primo, Pitch Black. The same week the page filled with singles. please wait on 17 October. run it back, try to love again, and outta this mess on 18 October. deeper on 19 October. safe as on 20 October. everyday on 23 October. The Avalanches refix that shared that date is not on the music list now. l i f e t i m e and w h a t ' s l u v on 24 October. The standalone don’t cry and the LT Memphis refix from that day are not on the music list now. A$AP FOREVER on 30 October. The Skin to Skin bootleg from 31 October is not on the music list now. Twelve titles. The Rose Rouge edit that was on this page on 18 October is on Archie’s page now, retitled and dated 10 November. The iPod Touch and So Good bootlegs that were on this page on 25 October are on Archie’s page now, retitled and dated 9 November. Outta Tha Woods, call anytime, and Freed From (Nanana) are not on the music page. A two-track single in August, then a run of one-track records in October, with Pitch Black the release grouped as an album.

The tags on the August single are the ones worth sitting with. Deep house, lo-fi house, and UK garage get filed as if they were separate rooms. Putting them on one short record describes how a lot of house gets made now: a dusty source, a UK swing, a patience about when the bass arrives. Whether these tracks earn every tag is testable. They are on the page. The October titles are plainer than the tags. safe as. deeper. please wait. try to love again. outta this mess. Any of them could be said across a kitchen.

Cumberland is a town on Vancouver Island. UK garage, as a tag, names a swing and a bass weight people learned from British records in the 1990s and have argued about since. Using it in 2026 on a Pacific Northwest account says which records this page wants to be heard beside. The bio does not expand on the choice. It states a town and a music. Played in release order, the page moves from a named single in August to a week in October with a new title on almost every day. That week has what a signing line never did: titles, dates, and a town that matches on the bio and the location line.
