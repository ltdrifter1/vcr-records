We Signed Rosco

Vancouver house that stays on after the room clears. We knew. Then we heard the drums lock at 3am and knew again.

Vancouver. We knew immediately — which is a smug sentence until you hear the drums lock and realize you would have signed the same email at 3am.

Rosco makes house that stays on after the room clears. Long loops. Low-end that feels like a hand, not a shove. Locked, finished, a little heartbroken in a way that still wants to dance. If you want a short way in, start with j'adore — the car, the dusk, the two tracks that refuse to drop you at the curb. If you want the whole picture, the self-titled cassette is the crossing.

He’s in. We’re not going to be cool about it.
