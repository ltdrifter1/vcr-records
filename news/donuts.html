Essential Listening: Donuts

Put this on when your loops feel too clean.

ListeningJul 8, 2025

CJ Archer

Put this on when your loops feel too clean.

Dilla finished a lot of Donuts while he was sick: MPC, stack of records, stubborn about feel. What’s useful isn’t the tragedy. It’s the ear. Swung hats. Late snare. “Off” made intentional.

Leave it running. Notice how little he needs.
