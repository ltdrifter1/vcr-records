The Mixtape Didn't Ask

FKA twigs put the club on a mixtape, and the kitchen already had the aux.

Date corrected on 22 October 2026. The previous date on this page fell before 12 February 2024, the founding date on the Club Copy about page.

FKA twigs didn’t wait for the album cycle to forgive her. CAPRISONGS landed as a mixtape, not a monument. “honda” is the window-cracked one already, rain on the hi-hats and Pa Salieu in the passenger seat, and “meta angel” is the come-up after MAGDALENE’s wound.

Then come “oh my love,” “pamplemousse,” and “tears in the club” with The Weeknd like a dare to the radio. This is not the funeral. This is the afters, and the kitchen already had the aux.

Eusexua Nobody Asked What to Put On Back issues

CJ Archer
