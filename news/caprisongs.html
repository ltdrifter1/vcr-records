The Mixtape Didn't Ask

FKA twigs put the club on a mixtape. The kitchen already had the aux.

posted by cj archer · Jan 14, 2022

FKA twigs didn’t wait for the album cycle to forgive her. CAPRISONGS landed as a mixtape, not a monument. “honda” is the window-cracked one already — rain on the hi-hats, Pa Salieu in the passenger seat. “meta angel” is the come-up after MAGDALENE’s wound.

“oh my love.” “pamplemousse.” “tears in the club” with The Weeknd like a dare to the radio. This is not the funeral. This is the afters. The kitchen already had the aux.
