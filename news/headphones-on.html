Headphones On

Addison’s debut is out. Twelve tracks, no features. Charli said trust it. I did on the walk home.

posted by cj archer

Addison Rae’s debut is out today. Twelve tracks, Columbia, no features. Elvira Anderfjärd and Luka Kloser produced the whole thing — “Diet Pepsi,” “Aquamarine,” “High Fashion,” “Headphones On,” “Fame Is a Gun.” The TikTok girl finished the club record. The group chat can stop pretending they weren’t already dancing.

Charli XCX told her to trust the ideas. Arca remixed “Aquamarine” into “Arcamarine” and then they did it at Coachella like a dare. Weekend two she screamed “Von Dutch” next to Charli and the desert remembered why Brat never actually ended. Tonight she’s at The Box in New York. A lot of people wanted a reason to stay mean about a dancer from Louisiana. The record didn’t give them one.

Trance-pop that sounds like a wet bathroom and a good jacket. I played “Headphones On” on the walk home and didn’t skip. I was mouthing it before I remembered I was supposed to have an opinion about TikTok. She didn’t ask the floor for permission. Fine. The aux was already hers.
