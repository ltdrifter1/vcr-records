LT Drifta on Club Copy

Eight titles on LT Drifta’s Bandcamp, from a standards album in 2025 to Shaolin Style on 24 October, plus the mixes on SoundCloud.

LT Drifta’s Bandcamp is specific about the town and short about the job. The location line says Cumberland, British Columbia. The bio says “Exploring the soulful side of electronics.” The music page lists eleven releases.

Classic Jazz came out on 4 October 2025. The titles read like a book of standards: Naima, My One and Only Love, Sentimental Mood, Four, Colors of You, Variations In Fall, Taste Honey. Naima is John Coltrane’s ballad. My One and Only Love is a standard. Sentimental Mood is the Ellington title with the first word left off. Four is the tune associated with Miles Davis. The page does not say these tracks are covers, and a shared name is not a sample. What the list does is sit an electronic album from a small town on Vancouver Island next to a repertoire jazz players still call. Colors of You, Variations In Fall, and Taste Honey have no famous twin. They keep the others company. He is willing to borrow the names.

Summer Madness followed on 14 November 2025. Eight tracks: Space and Time, Go!, Summer Madness, Too Far, Nu Saigon, You’re My, So What, Mood Indigo. So What and Mood Indigo bring Davis and Ellington back onto the sleeve, beside a title that sounds like a city and a title that is just a shout. Champion Sound, the single, is dated 15 December 2025 on the Bandcamp page for that track. Earlier copy on this site has given the same single a 2024 date. The date on ltdrifta.bandcamp.com, read for this piece, is 15 December 2025.

October 2026 moves faster. Three 4 Zone on the 18th. Nia Archives - Get Me Down (Molly’s Hazy Jungle Edit) on the 19th. The Chameleon - Links (L.T.’s VIP Mix) on the 23rd. Lavery - Shaolin Style (L.T.’s VIP Mix) on the 24th. For My Sins and Lenzman, Redeyes - High & Low (Remix) on the 25th. Madlib - Slims’ Return (LT’s Jungle Edit) on the 29th. Artificial Intelligence on the 30th. Omni Trio - Renegade Snares (L.T.’s VIP Mix) was on this page and is not on the music list now. The rest of those titles are on the Bandcamp music page, with the two albums from the autumn of 2025 and Champion Sound. The Nia Archives title does something the albums do not. It names another artist’s record, and it puts “jungle” in the parenthetical, next to Molly’s name. The October VIP mixes name other records the same way. On the album pages, jungle is not the word Bandcamp uses to file the music. It shows up when he is editing someone else.

The mixes are the other public half, and they are not for sale. The SoundCloud account, soundcloud.com/ltdrifta, describes itself as underground electronic music from the Pacific Northwest, location Cumberland. The uploads run in series: Deep in Tha Jungle, with years in the titles, Café Après, a shoegaze run called blissed out, Club Copy Radio, and NIGHTSHIFT, which shares a name with this magazine. NIGHTSHIFT Vol. 3 was posted on 2 October 2026. No tracklist is printed with it, so none is described here.

Two doors, then. Classic Jazz, if you want to see which tunes he is willing to name. The October singles, if you want what left the page this month. The mixes are where the longer listening went. Eleven titles on the music page and a long run of mixes, under a bio that stays modest: the soulful side of electronics. Lakeshore West 23:55 is on the label site and not on the Bandcamp music list.
