L.T. Drifta on Club Copy

Cumberland crate habit: jazz and soul flips, jungle when the speakers can take it, a 2 a.m. kettle that stays on.

posted by cj archer · May 3, 2024

L.T. Drifta lands on Club Copy the way a good guest lands after last call: coat half-off, bag of records implied, no speech. He is from Cumberland, which is not a branding choice. It is a crate habit. Jazz and soul still have fingerprints on the flips. Jungle shows up when the speakers can take it without anyone performing jungle for a camera. You can keep talking through the first two minutes. Then you are not talking, because the drums asked a question the conversation could not answer.

We recognized a habit before we recognized a bio. The 2 a.m. kettle. The flashlight in the shop. Lakeshore West 23:55 already sounded like a GO train you miss on purpose. Lions Gate, when it lands, will be named for the span that keeps you on the interesting side of the inlet. Champion Sound will be the quiet once you are inside. I am not going to pretend this is a trailer. It is a signing because the crate and the kettle were already the same person, and Copy House would have been lying to itself if we let him stay a guest.

Start with whatever is on the shelf the week you find this. The titles will pile up. The through-line will still be temperature, not a genre tag. I put the kettle on the night we made it official. That was the paperwork.
