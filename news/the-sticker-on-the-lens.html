The Sticker on the Lens

They banned phones at the door and still posted the story. The no-phone night is a flyer.

posted by cj archer · Oct 3, 2026

They put a sticker over my camera like it was a sacrament. No phones. Presence. The room as a room. Then the house photographer walked past with a lens the size of a wine bottle and the story went up before the taxi did. I still had glue on my phone at brunch.

New door policy: you can’t have the night, they can sell the night. Berghain had the rule first and meant it. Everybody else bought the sticker roll. 2012 the camera was a crime if you were in the photo. 2019 the story was the flyer. 2026 the flyer is a ban you photograph from the sidewalk. Promoters learned that “no phones” is a better headline than the DJ. Presence as branding. A room that asks you to be here, then here is a grid.

I don’t need to film a drop. I also don’t need a moral lecture from a brand account that posted the drop. If the sticker is for the room, put it on the staff cameras too. If it isn’t, just say you want the pictures and you don’t want ours. That’s honest. The stall already has a flashlight. The group chat already has the pin. The night will survive without my story. It will not survive another policy that only applies to the people who paid.
