The Group Chat Is the Door

RA is a brochure. The night lives in a thread that started at 4:12.

posted by cj archer

Resident Advisor is a brochure. Dice is a receipt. The night happens in a thread that started at 4:12 with are we still going. Someone has the guest list. Someone has the charger. Someone is already in the Uber eating a granola bar like it’s a moral position. The door does not know your name. The chat does.

This is not new. In 1998 it was three missed calls and a Motorola that died on the SkyTrain. In 2004 it was a mass text that said back door, tell them Maya. The technology got prettier. The politics stayed: who has the address, who has the cash, who will leave if the door is ugly. Women twenty-six and thirty-six run nights this way whether the flyer has a boy’s name on it or not.

The group chat is a door policy with better lighting. If you’re not in it, you can still go. You’ll just arrive like a tourist. Send the pin. Don’t add the guy who reviews rooms on his story. The floor already has enough landlords.

When the thread goes quiet, that’s not a maybe. That’s the afters starting without you. Put your shoes on or stay home and put Sade on. Both are a night. Only one requires a code.
