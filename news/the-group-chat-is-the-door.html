Are We Still Going

RA is a brochure. Dice is a receipt. The night starts at 4:12 in a thread.

posted by cj archer

Resident Advisor is a brochure. Dice is a receipt. The night happens in a thread that started at 4:12 with are we still going. Someone has the guest list. Someone has the charger. Someone is already in the Uber eating a granola bar like it’s a moral position. The door does not know your name. The chat does.

This isn’t new. In 1998 it was three missed calls and a Motorola that died on the SkyTrain. In 2004 it was a mass text that said back door, tell them Maya. The phones got prettier. The politics stayed: who has the address, who has the cash, who will leave if the door is ugly. Women twenty-six and thirty-six still run nights this way whether the flyer has a boy’s name on it or not.

If you’re not in the thread, you can still go. You’ll just arrive like a tourist. Send the pin. Don’t add the guy who reviews rooms on his story. When the chat goes quiet, that’s not a maybe. That’s the afters starting without you. Put your shoes on, or stay home and put Sade on. Both are a night. Only one needs a code.
