Feature

The List Stayed Short

Mark Ernestus opened Hard Wax in Kreuzberg in 1989, because Berlin had no shop that took acid and house seriously. A killer record moved 150 copies. The next one would not sell three. The descriptions stayed short enough to survive the building.

Contents

Essay

  1. Saved My Life i! Records issued Todd Edwards' Saved My Life in the US in 1995, catalogue IR-117, one side. He told Emma Warren in 2013 that an Ensoniq EPS held about 15 to 30 seconds, and that England sped the records up and called the result speed garage. 21 Nov 2026

Scene Report

  1. The Basement Holds 210 On 5 June 2012 David Tillson told Sunset Labs that Hush, at 1325 Government Street, held 210 people and booked with rent day in mind. The room closed in 2014. Across the strait, the Gore Avenue shop lasted about a year, and Mood Hut said the city limits had been the point. 21 Nov 2026

Review

  1. Ammnesia Jack Trax released Mr. Fingers' Ammnesia in the UK on 3 April 1989, catalogue FING 2, a letter short and licensed from Trax. Larry Heard had not authorized it. On 27 June 2022 his label Alleviated issued Amnesia, the spelling corrected. 21 Nov 2026
  2. The Unknown Writer Submerge issued Drexciya's The Quest in 1997, on double vinyl and on CD. The sleeve, signed The Unknown Writer, asked whether babies thrown from slave ships could have been born not needing air, and said the questions would not be answered. 21 Nov 2026

Horoscope

  1. Stars for the Floor One plain thing to finish before November is over, for every sign. 21 Nov 2026
