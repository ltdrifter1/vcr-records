The Last Emperor

Sakamoto, off the movie. I put it on when the apartment needs a temperature, not a plot.

CJ Archer

Sakamoto. The emperor, the snow, the room after. That’s the trick of the score from The Last Emperor — a century of ceremony, and still it sounds like someone thinking in the next room with the door half-open.

I don’t put it on for the Oscars story. I put it on when the apartment needs a temperature, not a plot. The themes move like memory: clear, a little distant, hard to rush. Sakamoto, Byrne, Cong Su wrote a score that actually listens. You can hear the space around the notes, which is the same space a good night needs.

Play it away from the movie once. Let the yellow banner on the sleeve be enough image. If it makes you sit down without meaning to, leave it on.
