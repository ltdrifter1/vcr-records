Mezzanine

Massive Attack, 1998. The 4am record. Not a costume. A temperature.

1998virginlp

posted by cj archer · Oct 31, 2026

Halloween is a costume. Mezzanine is not. I put it on when the apartment needs to feel like a wet street without going back outside. Massive Attack, 1998. Bristol. Bass that arrives like weather. A voice that sounds like it already left and came back anyway.

The boys filed it under trip-hop and moved to the next assignment. Fine. I file it under the hour when the kitchen is still a club and nobody is brave enough for something cheerful. “Teardrop” is the famous one and it still works if you don’t perform it. The rest of the record is the point — the long dark, the cigarette you don’t smoke, the window cracked because the night is in your hair. I have walked home to this. I have not walked home to this. Both counted.

If Love Deluxe is the apartment with people in it, Mezzanine is the apartment after they leave and you are not ready to be kind about it. Play it through. Don’t skip to the single. If you start the dishes in last night’s jersey, that’s the listening. October 31 can keep the costume. Leave this on. The room will tell you when to take it off.
