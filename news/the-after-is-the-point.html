The Afters Are the Point

The club is a rumor. The night that actually happens is the afters.

posted by cj archer

The photos from the club are a lighting plot. The night I remember starts when the coat hits a chair and somebody puts a record on without taking requests.

Queues became a personality. Booths became a brand. Monday became the point of Friday. Meanwhile the bathroom line decided if anyone was going to be kind, and the kitchen — one lamp, shoes off — was the only sound system you could actually live in.

Rosco named that hour Bridget In My Room. Door on the latch. Molly’s DESIRE is the same weather with better headphones. j'adore is the car that won’t drop you at the curb. None of them are trying to be a night out. They’re trying to be what the night out was for.

If a label only sounds like a Friday it missed the person who stayed. She’s the one buying the cassette.
