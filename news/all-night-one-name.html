All Night, One Name

Ibiza’s closing week gave Manda Moor, Mau P and Sirus Hood a room until morning. Everywhere else the poster split into pairs.

posted by cj archer · Oct 11, 2026

Three people were handed a room and told to stay in it. The rest of Ibiza’s last week was a poster that would not stop multiplying.

The bills had been public since August, when Hï and Ushuaïa posted their closing lineups. What 6 to 11 October put on the doors was two kinds of night at once. Tuesday at Hï, East End Dubs closed the first season of Eastenderz in the Theatre and Paco Osuna closed the Club Room. In the Wild Corner, the smallest room, Manda Moor played all night. One name, open to close.

Wednesday, Mau P closed Baddest Behaviour at Pacha the same way. All night, no second headliner, under the cherries. Jamie Jones closed Paradise the same night, after a second season at [UNVRS], with Adam Ten, Mason Maynard, SOSA and Manda Moor, a night after she had already played a room until morning. Downstairs at Paradise, the Bunker went to Hot Creations. Two closings, one island, and only one of them was a single person given the clock.

Thursday is the contrast inside one building. Upstairs at [UNVRS], Indira Paganotto presented ARTCORE with I Hate Models, Kobosil, AZYR and Anna Unusyan. Two nights earlier the DJ Mag poll had put her at 26 and handed her its first psy prize. I Hate Models had just fallen out of the hundred. Downstairs, Beatport Live gave the Bunker to Sirus Hood from open to close. Moor on Tuesday, Mau P on Wednesday, Hood on Thursday. The main room was a five-name bill. The room under it was one.

Friday is when the single name gives the room back. Hï’s Theatre went to Black Coffee, the Martinez Brothers, Paco Osuna and Delilah. The Club Room went to Skepta’s Más Tiempo, a crew. The Wild Corner, the room Moor had held alone three nights before, went to a full bill. In the room that had crowned him on Tuesday, Guetta closed Galactic Circus. Across the island, Glitterbox closed at Amnesia with Masters at Work, Dimitri from Paris, HoneyLuv and Jorja Smith live. Follow the rooms and Friday is the night the clock gets a committee.

Saturday, Ushuaïa gave its last party to ANTS and built eleven hours out of seven back-to-backs, a marathon whose unit is a pair. DJ Mag had published that shape in August. Amnesia billed the same Saturday as a 50th year, the club opened in 1976, and ran from the afternoon into Sunday. Joseph Capriati played at sunset, then back to back on the Terrace with Josh Baker, the poll’s highest new entry five days earlier, in at 40. The main room went to Klangkuenstler, Nina Kraviz, Deborah De Luca and Future666. De Luca had dropped 21 places, from 60 to 81, and had the big room anyway. Sunday, Destino Five closed Music On in the open air, Luciano among the names after his outstanding-contribution prize on Tuesday. Pacha finished with PAWSA and Miguelle & Tons. O Beach shut its season.

The gossip is those three boxes. Moor had the Wild Corner on Tuesday and a featured slot on Wednesday: an all-nighter buys you the next day, warm, and no longer alone in the room. Hood had the Bunker while five names played techno above him. Mau P had Pacha to himself, and that week’s poll had him up 22 places to 55, level with John Summit and Chris Stassy for the medal. The posters had promised scale since August. The all-night rooms had to be played in one sitting.

A night in Victoria does not get eleven hours of pairs, and a room in Portland has to give the street back before breakfast. What travels is the smaller dare. One person, the clock, no second headliner to hide behind. The part worth stealing is the Wild Corner.
