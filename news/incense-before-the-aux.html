Incense Before the Aux

Scent is the first DJ. The aux is late.

posted by cj archer

Before anybody touches the speaker, somebody lights something. Nag Champa from the head shop that also sold bad tarot. Cheap oil from Chinatown in a bottle with no English. A stick in a dish that used to be a candle. The apartment smells like the night before the night starts. Scent is the first DJ. The aux is late.

1999 the smoke was the whole aesthetic — tapestry, CD spindle, a girl doing her eyes in the toaster reflection. 2026 the incense is still there, next to the Bluetooth that keeps forgetting the kitchen. You can buy a perfume that costs more than the taxi and it still loses to the cheap stick if the cheap stick was on when people walked in. The body remembers smoke before it remembers a drop.

This is not wellness. This is stagecraft for a room that doesn’t have a stage. The afters live or die on whether the apartment already felt like a night when the first coat hit the chair. Put it on before they arrive. Don’t explain the scent. If they ask what it is, the night is already working.

If you hate incense, use the oil. If you hate the oil, use the candle that smells like a clean shirt and a lie. Something has to hit the doorway first. The aux can wait. It always does.
