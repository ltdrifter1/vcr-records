Essential Listening: Endtroducing

Still the door I send people through.

ListeningFeb 12, 2024

CJ Archer

Still the door I send people through.

If you make music out of other people’s records, you run into Endtroducing. Not as homework. As a room where someone is listening harder than most people talk.

Dig until the break surprises you. Leave space. Notice what you start hunting for afterward.
