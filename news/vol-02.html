Maxinquaye

Tricky’s debut album was released on 20 February 1995 by 4th & B’way. Martina Topley-Bird sings most of it. The record is not a Massive Attack album with one member stepped forward.

Some pieces in this volume were published on 21 October 2026 and filed here because the month was thin. The file strip on those pages gives that date.

Essay

  1. Hymn of the Big Wheel Massive Attack issued the single, backed with “Be Thankful for What You’ve Got,” on 10 February 1992. Blue Lines was already ten months old. 21 Oct 2026
  2. Shook Ones, Part II Mobb Deep released the single on 7 February 1995. The album it comes from, The Infamous, followed on 25 April. Havoc produced most of that album. Q-Tip is also credited. 21 Oct 2026
  3. Unfinished Sympathy The single came out on 11 February 1991. Blue Lines, the album it is on, came out on 8 April. The song was public before the record that now contains it. 21 Oct 2026
  4. Geogaddi Boards of Canada’s second studio album is listed as 13 February 2002, on Warp. The UK albums chart peak on that discography is 21. BPI: silver. 21 Oct 2026
