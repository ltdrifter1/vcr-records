Double-Edge Returns

Double-Edge’s next album, Double-Edge Returns, is up for digital pre-order on Club Copy. It’s a full-length of jungle — sharp breaks, heavy low end, and the kind of late-night pressure he’s been building across his Club Copy releases.

Pre-order now and you’ll get the files by email when the album lands. Until then, Enter, Double-Edge is a good place to start if you haven’t already.
