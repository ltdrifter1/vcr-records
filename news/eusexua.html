When a Girl Feels Good

FKA twigs coined a word for the floor. The girls who dance already had the feeling.

posted by cj archer

She made a word for it. Eusexua — the second before you come back into your body, the Prague bathroom, the techno that doesn’t need Berlin to explain itself. Album three, out today, and the group chat is already arguing about whether “Girl Feels Good” is a hymn or a dare. Both. When a girl feels good it makes the world go round is not a caption. It’s the weather the floor has been trying to name since we were nineteen and somebody finally turned the lights the right colour.

Brat summer taught a lot of people the club as a font. Twigs is doing the other job: the club as a nervous system. “Drums of Death” was built on a flight to Berghain and sounds like it. “Room of Fools” is the somatic one — you don’t raise your arms, you remember you have them. The dress code for the raves was scorched soil and steel. In Cumberland that’s a wet jacket and the good boots. Same religion. Different climate.

The gossip isn’t that a pop star “went techno.” The gossip is that the record doesn’t insult the rooms it borrowed. Play “Girl Feels Good” with the window cracked and nobody talking. If your shoulders drop before the chorus, that’s the review. She named the state. You were already in it.
