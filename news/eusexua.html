When a Girl Feels Good

FKA twigs coined a word for the floor. The girls who dance already had the feeling.

posted by cj archer

FKA twigs made a word for the second before you come back into your body. Eusexua. Prague bathroom. Techno that doesn’t need Berlin to explain itself. Album three. “Girl Feels Good” is already splitting the group chat into hymn versus dare. It’s both.

Brat summer taught a lot of people the club as a font. This record is doing the other job. “Drums of Death” was built on a flight to Berghain. “Room of Fools” is the somatic one. You don’t have to like the coinage. You already had the feeling.
