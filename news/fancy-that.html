Fancy That

PinkPantheress dropped a 20-minute mixtape. UK garage, no outro, no features.

posted by cj archer

PinkPantheress released Fancy That today — nine tracks, twenty minutes and change, Warner UK. No features. Garage, jungle, four-on-the-floor that still sounds like a bedroom even when it wants a club. She has said, more than once, that we don’t need a long outro. The mixtape is the argument.

Singles “Tonight” and “Stateside” already did the rounds. The Dare is in the credits on the latter. Samples and interpolations do the rest: Basement Jaxx, Underworld’s “Dark & Long,” Adina Howard via Sugababes, William Orbit. UK pop as a crate, not a costume. After Heaven Knows this is the shorter, louder one — Clubland BPM with diary posture, the sound a lot of girls in this scene have been trying to name without saying “Y2K” like a moodboard.

Street date is now. Twenty minutes is a commute, a shower, the walk from the bar to the car. Put it on once through without skipping. If you’re still in it when it stops, that’s the news. The summer aux just got a runtime.
