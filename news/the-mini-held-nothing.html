The Mini Held Nothing

Bag the size of a tin. Lip, key, phone in the boot. Boys asked where she put her stuff.

posted by cj archer · Nov 21, 2026

The bag holds almost nothing on purpose. A lip. A key. A lie about having somewhere to put the phone. The phone is in the boot. The card is in the case. The stall will hold the rest. I watched a girl at the cloakroom try to check a bag that wasn’t a bag and the boy behind the counter looked personally betrayed.

2003 it was a pocket in low-rise everything and a lip gloss that leaked. 2014 it was a backpack you swore was ironic. This year the mini is the fit. Not a utility argument. A policy: I am not carrying your night. Cute as armor, again, just smaller. The petition class will call it impractical. The night is impractical. That’s why you left the apartment.

Hold her drink. Don’t ask where the phone is. If she has to go back to the boot between tracks, you wait. The xerox of the evening is already on the collarbone. The bag was never for stuff. It was for the photograph of not having stuff. Wear it. Or don’t. The stall still has a lock either way.
