The Floor Already Knew

Honey Dijon’s Black Girl Magic is house as a family affair. Eve, the club, and a kick that doesn’t apologize.

posted by cj archer

Honey Dijon’s Black Girl Magic is out on Classic Music Company. Chicago to the world. “Work.” “In the Club” with Eve. Pabllo Vittar and Urias on “Everybody.” Channel Tres. Mike Dunn. Luke Solomon. A family affair with a kick that still means something at 4am.

She did not come to rescue house. She has been in the booth this whole time, four-on-the-floor while everybody else was doing livestreams in their kitchen. Second album. It feels like a night she designed and then let you into.
