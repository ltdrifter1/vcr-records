She Showed Up

Lorde walked into a night thrown in her name. Management wanted the green room. She wanted the floor.

posted by cj archer

Sunday in Sydney. Mary’s Underground throws a Lorde night — What Was That, drag, the drinks named after the era — and then she is actually there. Not a stunt the venue planned. The Kiwi walked in, skipped the green room, stood in the crush, sang along to her own song like a girl who still goes out. When they tried to extract her she came back. That’s the clip. That’s the gossip. Every woman in this province who has ever DJed a dedicated night for a singer she loved felt the floor tilt.

“What Was That” is the first dance-pop she’s given us since Melodrama, which is to say: the MDMA record, the coloured light, the one we still put on when the kitchen is being honest. Washington Square got shut down by cops a month ago. Baby’s All Right is in the lyrics. Virgin lands June 27. Jim-E Stack. Dan Nigro. New York breakup, late 2023, the kind of detail that makes a certain listener cancel plans.

The underground doesn’t need her to be underground. It needs her to still want the room more than the photograph of the room. Last night she did. Play it on the drive home from a bar that isn’t Mary’s and isn’t Sydney. If you start singing before the second chorus and forget to perform irony, that’s the review. She showed up. The girls who throw the party were already there.
