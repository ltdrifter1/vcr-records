It Goes Like Nanana

Peggy Gou wrote the summer. The field already knew the rest.

posted by cj archer

Peggy Gou’s “(It Goes Like) Nanana” landed on XL and by Sunday it was the aux between here and the gorge. Not a drop. A whistle you already knew how to do. Italo sunshine with house posture.

She’s been the booth for years — Ninja Tune 12s, fashion-week afters, no gimmick required. Then a melody walked out of the club and into the field. You can be precious about crossover. You can also admit a hook this clean is why people still go outside.
