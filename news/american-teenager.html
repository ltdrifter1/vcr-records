American Teenager

Ethel Cain’s Preacher’s Daughter is the highway record. Church, motels, someone who kept the aux.

posted by cj archer · May 12, 2022

Preacher’s Daughter arrived and “American Teenager” was already stuck in the throat. Ethel Cain didn’t write a vibes album. She wrote a southern gothic you can drive: motels, diners, a Harley in a parking lot.

“Gibson Girl.” “Strangers.” “Sun Bleached Flies.” It’s long. It should be. She came out, the church made it a problem, and the record treats that as weather — not a press angle. You don’t shuffle this. You take the highway at dusk and you let “Strangers” finish.
