Essential Listening: Ahmad Jamal '73

CJ Archer

The Rhodes was a gift from Switzerland.

Most people meet Ahmad Jamal through the acoustic stuff. Dig further and you hit Ahmad Jamal '73: all Fender Rhodes, covers that float, originals that still turn up in beatmaker crates.

He took a personal gesture and made a whole record speak electric. Same ear for space. Different voltage. I keep coming back to it.
