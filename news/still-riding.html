Still Riding

Barry Can't Swim, rewritten for the ride home. Same day as DESIRE.

ReleaseSep 7, 2026

Some edits feel like homework. This one feels like somebody borrowed your aux and refused to give it back, in a good way.

Molly Haze took Barry Can't Swim’s Still Riding and kept the romance — that sense of moving through a city that still likes you — then lengthened the night around it. Six minutes thirty-three. Digital only. Out the same day as DESIRE, which is the original-mix sister: one record for the room, one for the wet street after.

I like an edit that doesn’t show you the seams. You don’t need to know what got moved. You need to know that the last chorus arrives like a streetlight you didn’t realize you’d been walking toward. Play it in the car. Play it again when you’re already home and not ready to be.
