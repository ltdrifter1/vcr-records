Still Riding

Molly’s Barry Can't Swim edit. Some edits feel like homework. This one stole the aux and kept it.

Some edits feel like homework. This one feels like somebody borrowed your aux and would not give it back, and you were glad.

Molly Haze took Barry Can't Swim’s Still Riding and kept the romance — that sense of moving through a city that still likes you — then lengthened the night around it. Six minutes thirty-three. Digital only. Out the same day as DESIRE, which is the original-mix sister: one record for the room, one for the wet street after. I have been putting this one on in the car even when I am only going to the shop.

I like an edit that doesn’t show you the seams. You don’t need to know what got moved. You need the last chorus to arrive like a streetlight you didn’t realize you’d been walking toward. Play it on the way. Play it again when you’re already home and not ready to be.
