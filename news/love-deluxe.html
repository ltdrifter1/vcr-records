Love Deluxe

The apartment record. Put it on when you want the room to like you back.

ListeningSep 4, 2026

posted by cj archer

The crate column in this zine has been boys’ homework for too long — Dilla, Shadow, Jamal, all of it true, all of it a certain kind of listening. Here’s the record you actually leave on. Sade, Love Deluxe, 1992. Not a museum piece. An apartment.

You know the temperature even if you think you don’t. Bass that sits like a hand on the small of your back. A voice that refuses to raise itself to be believed. No drop, no proof, no “and then.” It’s the sound of someone who already knows you came over. I put it on when the house is mine and when it isn’t. It doesn’t care if you’re being brave about it. It just keeps the lights the color of almost.

Half the Club Copy catalog is trying to get back to this without saying her name — Molly’s private mix, Rosco’s latch on the door, the kitchen after the club. Love Deluxe is the original of that feeling. Play “No Ordinary Love” once without performing it for anyone. Then leave the album running. If you start moving the room around — a candle, a jacket off a chair, a window — that’s the review. That’s the listening we should have led with.
