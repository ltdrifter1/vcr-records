She Called It the Summer of Jungle

Nia Archives dropped Forbidden Feelingz. Bradford, Maya Angelou, and a tempo the field already missed.

posted by cj archer

Nia Archives put Forbidden Feelingz out on her own label, HIJINXX. Bradford. West Indian. A grandma sample and a break that doesn’t ask London for permission. “Ode 2 Maya Angelou” is not a citation. It’s a poet in a rave where the old heads said the girls don’t play jungle like that anymore.

They were wrong. “Forbidden Feelingz” has the ragged 90s tape hiss the originators kept in a crate and the vocal the new rooms actually need. Congo Natty already knows. She didn’t polish the breaks into drum-and-bass furniture. She bit.
