The Night Still Belongs to Us

By CJ Archer

A good track doesn’t ask for attention. It takes the air. You feel it in the chest first — then the mind shows up late and tries to explain what the feet already knew.

Jungle said the future is already breaking. House said we can rebuild a room out of pulse and mercy. Both still hold. The night doesn’t die — it relocates. Warehouse, headphones on the bus, a bedroom in Cumberland where someone’s still shaping a kick.

Double-Edge works the pressure. Inlet Knight works the quieter hour after the room empties. Same coast, different weather.

Stay late. Leave room for the thing that can’t be posted — only felt, then kept.
