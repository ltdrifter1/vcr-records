I’m Outside

The curb is a room. The text is the door policy. Don’t make her wait in the rain while you find a jacket.

posted by cj archer · May 22, 2026

The night has a last room before the room. The curb. A Honda with the hazards on. i’m outside at 11:47 while you are still doing your mouth in a toaster reflection. She is not your driver. She is the liturgy. You come down.

1998 it was three missed calls and a Motorola dying on the SkyTrain. 2004 it was a horn you weren’t supposed to use in that neighbourhood. 2026 it is a blue bubble and a live location you will ignore because you are looking for the other boot. The rain here does not care about your eyeliner. The ritual is shoes, jacket, keys, down the stairs like you meant it. Do not send five mins unless it is five mins. Do not make her loop the block while you change the song.

If you are the one on the curb, you already did the generous thing. If you are the one upstairs, the night started without you. Put the lip in your pocket. The aux can wait in the car. It always does.
