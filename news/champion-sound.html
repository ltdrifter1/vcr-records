Champion Sound

If Lions' Gate was the crossing, this is L.T. Drifta once you’re inside.

You know that feeling — you made it across, the city’s still lit, and nobody needs you to explain why you stayed out. That’s this record.

If Lions' Gate was the night crossing, Champion Sound is what L.T. Drifta does once you’re inside. Crate heat. Jazz in the flips. Sessions that don’t apologize for staying quiet, which is its own kind of confidence. I like a record that treats the afters as the main event.

Leave it on. Let the last track decide when you’re done.
