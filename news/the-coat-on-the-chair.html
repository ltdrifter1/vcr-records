The Coat on the Chair

The going-out top stayed on. The coat never left the kitchen. There was no cloakroom to prove it.

posted by cj archer

The floor is the photograph. The kitchen is the edit. June 2021, a patio rumour, a going-out top under a hoodie, platforms on linoleum. You got dressed like the door might exist. The coat went on the chair because there was no cloakroom and you were not taking it to the bedroom like a civilian. Cute as armor did not start on TikTok. It started when the only person who saw the fit was the group chat and the girl on the other bunk.

1999 you left the jacket with a stranger and prayed. 2003 the camera phone was still a rumour and the lip gloss lived in the pocket of low-rise everything. This year the stall is your own bathroom and the light is still criminal. You still redraw the lip between tracks. You still decide if you’re staying, except staying means staying. The night is the apartment. The fit is the proof you intended to leave.

Keep the coat on the chair. Don’t fold it. Folding it means you admitted nobody was coming. If she shows up in the thing she bought in March 2020, you tell her. That’s the whole review. The xerox of the evening is already on the collar. The street can wait.
