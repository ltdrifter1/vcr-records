Share the Fall

Roni Size & Reprazent released the single on 19 May 1997. It reached number 37 in the UK in June. The album New Forms followed on 23 June.

New Forms, the debut album by Roni Size & Reprazent, is a June release and is filed in the June volume. The lead single is May. “Share the Fall” came out on 19 May 1997 and reached number 37 on the UK singles chart in June, according to the album’s Wikipedia page. A 1996 EP, Reasons for Sharing, had already contained the song, along with “Down,” “Trust Me,” and “Sounds Fresh.” Three of those four were carried onto the album. The May date is therefore not the song’s first appearance. It is the date of the single that introduced the album campaign. First appearance and lead single are different events. The EP is 1996. The single is 19 May 1997. The album is 23 June 1997.

The producers of the album are Roni Size, DJ Die, Krust, and DJ Suv. The label is Talkin’ Loud, with later reissues on Mercury and Universal. None of that personnel detail belongs exclusively to the single, and this piece will not invent a separate production credit for the 12-inch. What it will say is that the single charted, modestly, in the month of the album, from a release dated the month before. Number 37 is not a smash. It is a chart position high enough to be a public fact and low enough to stop the legend that the group arrived as pop. They arrived, on this single, as a top-forty record in a dance market that had room for jungle-derived music at that height and no obligation to take it higher.

Reasons for Sharing is the underground object. “Share the Fall” as a Talkin’ Loud single is the object with a number. The distance between an EP in 1996 and a chart single in May 1997 is the distance between a scene pressing its own records and a label campaign. Both can be true of the same song. The song does not have to be embarrassed by the chart, and the chart position does not have to be inflated into a cultural victory. Thirty-seven is thirty-seven. The album that followed peaked at number 8 and stayed on the UK albums chart for 46 weeks. The single’s job was smaller. It was the knock on the door. The album, in June, is the room.

Filing the single in May and the album in June is how a monthly magazine avoids the habit of letting the famous object swallow its own campaign. Anyone who only reads the June piece will be told the single’s date. Anyone who reads this page gets the date at full length, including the EP that came before the campaign. Roni Size & Reprazent are a drum and bass group on the album page’s wording. The single is the first 1997 object in that wording’s public life. May holds it. June holds the double album it was pointing at.
