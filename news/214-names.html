214 Names on the Guest List

Ibiza booked 980 artist slots. Only 214 went to women and gender-diverse DJs. The island called that an improvement.

posted by cj archer

Ibiza published the summer fantasy again: white walls, black sunglasses, forty-foot LED, everybody beautiful enough to look accidental. Then Laureline Teste counted the names. Across 980 bookings at major 2025 residencies, 214 went to women and gender-diverse artists. Seven hundred and sixty-six went to men. Twenty-two percent, sold back as a slight improvement.

The ugly part is not one cartoon villain at one velvet rope. It is repetition. Marco Carola’s Pacha residency came in at 9.7 percent women and gender-diverse bookings. The Martinez Brothers at Hï and Bedouin at Chinois each sat at 12.1 percent. CircoLoco — a brand whose cool has travelled far beyond DC-10 — reached 19 percent. Men invite the men they know, those men become the safe ticket, and the same group photo gets another season.

Even women-led residencies did not automatically fix the room. Resident Advisor’s review counted six women against twenty-six men for BLOND:ISH at Pacha, and five against thirty-three for MËSTIZA at Hï. Visibility at the top does not trickle down by magic. A woman on the billboard can still be standing in front of a mostly male spreadsheet.

The better numbers were not parity either. Pyramid at Amnesia, Francis Mercier’s Solèy at Chinois, Carl Cox at UNVRS and Defected at Pacha placed roughly a third of slots with women and gender-diverse artists. That is enough to prove the talent pool exists and not enough to call the work finished.

So keep the number in your Notes app: 214 of 980. Use it when the lineup drops and somebody says there just were not enough names. There were names. They were making the records, running the labels, closing smaller rooms and getting placed in side rooms. The island did not have a supply problem. It had a guest-list problem.

Filed from Resident Advisor, 2025 Ibiza booking analysis
