Stars for the Floor

This week’s horoscope, for whoever is still out.

posted by cj archer · Sep 10, 2026

I used to steal my roommate’s Nylon for the horoscope. Not the reviews. The back page, next to the perfume sample, typeface like a note passed in class. Jane did it. The Face pretended not to, then ran one anyway. Nobody in the art department believed Mercury. You read it in the kitchen while the kettle went, still in last night’s mascara, and it told you whether to wear the thing.

One shoe is still in the hall and the lip gloss on the counter never got the cap back on, which is the only clock this paper trusts. Take the line with your name on it.

this week on the floor

Ariesmar 21 – apr 19fire
Put the heel in the bag and the boot on the foot. If the door looks twice, that was the point.
Taurusapr 20 – may 20earth
Twelve dollars, bent corner, yours. The person on their phone does not get the price.
Geminimay 21 – jun 20air
Two lipsticks in the one pocket. The darker shade is for the address you have not texted.
Cancerjun 21 – jul 22water
The kettle goes on before you leave. The chipped mug is for whoever comes back with you.
Leojul 23 – aug 22fire
The bouncer has your name from a list you wrote. Walk in as if that list were a rumor.
Virgoaug 23 – sep 22earth
The cross-out on the set list can live. Do your mouth in the cab, under the little light.
Librasep 23 – oct 22air
Hand over the aux at the light on Kingsway. You defended that song for six blocks and lost.
Scorpiooct 23 – nov 21water
If they ask who played, describe the hands. The name stays in your pocket with the lighter.
Sagittariusnov 22 – dec 21fire
Last train is a rumor and the rain is not. Walk while your hair still looks on purpose.
Capricorndec 22 – jan 19earth
Staple last night’s wristband to the page. Your version will be prettier than the paper.
Aquariusjan 20 – feb 18air
Three of them are still typing and you are already on the corner. Send that, not another poll.
Piscesfeb 19 – mar 20water
Body Shop, behind the ear, one dab. The kitchen light does not get a vote.

If your sign feels wrong, borrow the other sign’s. Nobody at the door is checking a birth chart. The bathroom line already decided who you are tonight.
