Stars for the Floor

This week’s horoscope.

posted by cj archer

Nylon ran stars next to the record reviews. Jane did it in the back, in a typeface that looked like a note passed in class. The Face pretended not to, then did it anyway. Not because anyone in the art department believed Mercury. Because you read it in the kitchen while the kettle went, still in last night’s mascara, and it told you whether to wear the thing.

Night Shift is an evening paper, not a lifestyle book. Even evening papers used to keep a column for the people who actually bought the issue. Here is this week for the floor, the fit, the afters. Take what you need. Leave the rest on the counter with the lip gloss.

Aries
Wear the thing that scares the door. If they clock it, you’re in.
Taurus
Buy the object. Flip it. Don’t explain the receipt.
Gemini
Two rooms. One outfit. Don’t text the first one from the second.
Cancer
Put Sade on before anyone asks. The afters are already in your kitchen.
Leo
You are the flyer. Don’t wait to be introduced to your own night.
Virgo
The playlist is fine. Stop adding one more. Go do your mouth.
Libra
Let her pick the Uber aux. You picked last week.
Scorpio
Don’t tell them the name of the night. They’ll find it if they should.
Sagittarius
Leave before the lights. The sidewalk version is better anyway.
Capricorn
Pay for the cab. Keep the receipt in the jacket you’ll wear again.
Aquarius
The group chat isn’t a vote. Send the pin.
Pisces
Glitter on the collarbone is a policy. Enforce it.

If your sign feels wrong, wear the other girl’s. The door cannot fact-check a birth chart. The bathroom line already decided.
