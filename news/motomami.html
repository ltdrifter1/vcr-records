The Bike, Not the Ballad

Rosalía dropped MOTOMAMI. Saoko, chrome, and a record that refuses the costume.

posted by cj archer

MOTOMAMI does not care if you wanted another ballad for a wedding slideshow. “SAOKO.” “CHICKEN TERIYAKI.” “BIZCOCHITO.” She transformed, then she transformed again, then she refused to sit still long enough for a genre sticker on the tank.

“LA FAMA” with The Weeknd is the trapdoor. “HENTAI” is the one you play when the group chat is arguing about whether she’s still flamenco. She was never your flamenco. She treated tradition like a bike you actually ride.
