LT Drifta

Artist · Producer Jungle Hip-Hop Electronic
Bandcamp Instagram SoundCloud

LT Drifta builds worlds from broken rhythm and late-night haze — jungle breaks colliding with hip-hop weight, cassette grit meeting digital precision.

As a core voice of VCR Recordings, the project moves between club pressure and headphone intimacy: dusty samples, rolling amen energy, and melodic fragments that feel half-remembered. Releases like Champion Sound, Summer Madness, and Lions' Gate map a catalog that treats physical formats as part of the story — limited cassettes, full artwork, and music meant to be lived with.

Less a brand than a frequency: underground electronic music with a human pulse.
